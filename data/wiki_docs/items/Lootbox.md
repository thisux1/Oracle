# Lootbox

- Lootboxes** (or lbs) are single-use items that can give you materials, mob drops (Ver: Drops#Hunt%20Drops), epic currency, and/or time travel (Ver: Time Travel) bonuses. The loot that you can receive from a lootbox depends on the tier of the lootbox and luck. You can purchase a lootbox from the shop, through voting for the bot or obtain them as a drop from hunt and adventure.

The cooldown (Ver: Cooldowns) for buying a lootbox is 3 hours and you can hold as many lootboxes in your inventory as you want. However, note that lootboxes are lost when you time travel (Ver: Time Travel). 

You cannot get items in a lootbox which are unlocked in a higher area than your max area (e.g. you cannot get apples or zombie eyes from lootboxes until you reach area 3). **The only exception to this are logs and fish**. [See drops for a full list of which items unlock where.]

1. # Commands

      - /buy [lootbox name]**
    - Purchases a lootbox from the shop.
    - Ex: "rpg buy edgy lootbox" or "rpg buy ed lb"
      - /open - /use [lootbox name] [amount]**
    - Opens the lootbox(es) currently held in the player's inventory. Must specify the lootbox name.

Note: Finding the time capsule from godly lootbox or VOID lootbox instantly increases player's time travel bonus upon usage but does not reset their current profile or inventory.

Getting a lootbox chain (i.e. opening an edgy lootbox and receiving an epic, then opening the epic to get rare, etc.) is possible but the chance is ~0.06%.

There is a chance that while trying to open a lootbox, it will appear to be stuck and it will initiate the lootbox event (Ver: Events#Lootbox Event). This will not happened if you are opening 100+ lootbox at once

1. # Trivia
Prior to the horse festival update on August 23rd UTC, the Epic coins and mega logs were common drops from the Omega lootbox while epic fish and hyper log were uncommon drops. All else remain unchanged. 

From the godly lootbox, the only uncommon drop prior to the horse festival update was the ultra log.
