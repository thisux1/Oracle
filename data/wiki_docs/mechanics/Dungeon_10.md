# Dungeon 10

Congrats on reaching dungeon 10!! Now this is the part where it gets a little weird, but no worries, this is one of the easiest dungeons there are because it is all scripted ;D


Dungeon 10 can **ONLY** be attempted by **2** players, no more no less. Both players ***must*** have a full edgy set (both edgy sword and edgy armor). Otherwise, you will not be allowed into the dungeon. (NOTE: you can attempt the dungeon with forged gear such as ultra edgy sword and ultra edgy armor.)  Because this dungeon is scripted, both of you will designate who will get the attacker role and who will get the defender role.  

Note: The first player can be either Defender or Attacker, second player will go with the other role.  

    - Tip:** Once you have reached area 10, you will have access to the command **/start advanced**, which will list the requirements for Dungeons 10-14, 15.1 and 15.2.

1. #  **Attacker Role** 
Your job is simple but important: charge your edgy sword until it reaches 100%. Once it reaches 100%, that means **ATTACK**! The dragon will have 1HP but do not let that fool you into attacking too early. Do not attack too late either as the dragon will kill you before you can perform the attack. So while this role is simple, please pay absolute attention to your charge percentage (%). Overcharging (charging past 100%) is possible and *will* result in dungeon failure.

    - Here is the recommended order: Charge Edgy Sword (x20), Attack**


1. #  **Defender Role** 
This job is slightly more complicated but fun, and the script has already been written so there is not much need to worry unless you mess up the order. The defender is in charge of protecting the attacker while taking the brunt of the attacks so do not worry when you die, because you ***will*** die in order to win.

    - Here is the recommended order: Weakness Spell, Protect, Charge Edgy Armor (x2), Protect (Until 10HP), Invulnerability, Healing Spell, Protect (x5)**

    - Alternative order: Weakness Spell, Protect (x3), Charge Edgy Armor (x2), Invulnerability, Healing Spell, Protect (x5)**
