# Shop

Shop is one of the places where players can obtain basic items, dungeon keys, lootboxes, boosts and lottery tickets. Buying and selling of some items is rewarded with a progress towards Merchant (Ver: Professions#Merchant) profession.

1. #  **Shop commands** 
Shop is the place that sells items for coins.

1. ##  **Shop** 
There are 2 pages of the shop which can be accessed with the following commands: 
- **/shop**
- **/shop 2**

1. ##  **Buy** 
Players can buy standard shop and epic shop items with the **/buy [item name]** command.

Multiple items can be bought with **/buy [item name] [quantity]**.

1. ###  Epic jumps 
You unlock epic jumps in the shop for the price of 2 time dragon essences by successfully defeating the EPIC NPC in the TOP (EPIC NPC fight (Ver: The TOP)).

1. ##  **Sell** 
Player can sell inventory items with the **/sell [item name]** command.
 PLAYER: /sell wooden log
 
 
 EPIC RPG [BOT]: 1 wooden log successfully sold for 25 coins
Multiple items can be sold with **rpg sell [item name] [quantity]**.
 PLAYER: /sell wooden log 100
 
 
 EPIC RPG [BOT]: 100 wooden log successfully sold for 2,500 coins

1. ##  **Help** 
You can check out each item information and sell value with **help**, command: **/help [item name]**.

 PLAYER: /h wooden log

1. #  **Epic Shop** 
Epic shop is the place that sells items for EPIC coins. You can gain EPIC coins via random drops, lootboxes, special events, daily streak of 7+, giveaways, or by purchasing them via donating and/or high Patreon tiers.

These items (except the horse token) have a shared cooldown of 30 min!

To get list of the epic items sold, use **/epic** **shop**.

Players can buy any of the epic shop items with the **/buy [item name]** command.
