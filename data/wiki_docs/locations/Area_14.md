1. #  Area 14 
You've defeated one of the most complicated dungeons in the game, whether it was in legitimate ways or not, it doesn't matter. You are here, the final point standing you from Area 15, but it will take you a while to actually challenge the GODLY dragon. You wanna continue working on your professions, mostly merchant. If you manage to max it, then start working on Worker, by cooking banana pickaxes each end of your run.

You unlock adventure hardmode, which works exactly the same as hunt hardmode.

1. #  Time Travel 10+ 
You can finally challenge the GODLY Dragon, a hard dungeon, but a really fun one. You want a **lot** of health for this Dungeon, as you will be taking damage from all sides.

1. ##  **Recommendations & Requirements** 
It is required to have at least 10+ Time Travels, OMEGA Armor and Dungeon Key / Tier VI+ Horse

The recommended HP is 2095 and Life Boost C and 1159 DEF.

As this dungeon is decently hard, you should check out a **Guide** (Ver: Dungeon 14)
