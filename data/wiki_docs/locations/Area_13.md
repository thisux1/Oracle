1. # Area 13
Welcome to Area 13, After defeating the OMEGA boss, you're rewarded with one of the best commands in the game, hunt hardmode (Ver: Hunt). Hunt hardmode increases your chances of getting a mob drop, increases gold and XP and it increases the average lootbox tier. Although not for free, because monsters are way stronger so be careful! You also unlock transmute (Ver: Enchant#Command%20Tiers), a far more powerful version of enchant and refine. 

If you have between 3 to 5 Time Travels (Ver: Time Travel), you should look to Time Travel as soon as possible. At this point your Crafter should either be nearly maxed or maxed, so look into working in Merchant with the remaining mats.

It is recommended to level up Merchant (Ver: Professions#Merchant) by selling wooden logs, you can sell in batches, and repeat this until you get Merchant maxed.

1. # Time Travel 5+
Be careful, this Dungeon is by far one of the most complicated in the entire game, it is completely based on strategy, this dungeon is based completely on HP. 

The requirements are 

- 5+ Time Travels
- OMEGA Sword
- Dungeon Key or Tier VI+ Horse

As for your health, you want to have 951 + Life Boost C (1001 HP, just enough to forge OMEGA sword)
