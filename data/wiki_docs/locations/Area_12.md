1. #  TT1-TT2 Guide 
This is your Time Travel Area, with the leftover materials you want to have at least around 30 million gold, though this is not as important anymore as pets can bring a lot of gold. Use all leftover materials remaining to upgrade your Crafter (Ver: Professions#Crafter) profession, the method remains the same as mentioned in Area 11 page.

Sell all your crops, equipment, life potions and then initiate a Time Travel!

1. ##  **Time Travel 3+ Guide** 
Dungeon 12 is your next Dungeon, it is less luck reliant and more strategy based so you need to think your moves. Unlike Dungeon 11, which is based around Attack and HP, Dungeon 12 is based on Defense and HP. Use same method as Area 11 to gain HP through Fruit Salads or Baked Fish.

Ultra-Edgy Armor (Ver: Crafting#Forging) is **required** for this Dungeon, so make sure to craft it! 

There are no recommended trades.  The recommended working commands (Ver: Working Commands) is Chainsaw for materials and Dynamite for coins.
