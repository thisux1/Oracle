Typhon|image=Dungeon_9.webp|nextarea=Area 10}}<p webmemes_parsed="1">**Area** is the 9th area of Epic RPG. In this area you'll get access to chips from Killer robots, to get the final mob drops to craft the legendary Edgy Armor! You're nearing the end of your first journey, for your first time travel. 
1. # Stat Recommendation for D9
1. ## **Recommended stats**
- Attack: 282
- Defense: 455 (you get this easily with edgy armor, Carry def is 330!)
- Life: 370
1. ## **Recommended Grinding**
Continue hunting to get chips in order to craft your Edgy armor using the mob drops you previously acquired, continue adventuring, doing working commands, farms and keep training! Once you have edgy armor it should be a piece of cake.

1. ## **Recommended Gear**
- Sword: Coin Sword (Hyper enchant or above)
- Armor: Edgy Armor (Hyper enchant or above)
- Level: 55
1. ## **Recommended Trades**
Dismantle epic logs and bananas, trade all your mats to logs and trade your logs to fishes.
