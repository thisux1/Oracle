Typhon|image=Dungeon 10.webp|nextarea=Area 11}}**Area 10** is the key point for begginers. This is it, the last dungeon, until you can finally Time Travel. All you've been working for was meant for this moment. You should already have the EDGY Armor (Ver: Crafting#Armors) and the materials to make an EDGY Sword (Ver: Crafting#Swords) as both of these gears are **required** to enter the dungeon.

You should have a good amount of leftover materials, which will soon be used for professions XP or money.  It is recommended to dismantle all bananas and trade all apples into logs.
