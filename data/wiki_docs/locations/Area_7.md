1. # Stat Recommendations for D7
1. ## **Recommended Stats**
- Attack: 257
- Defense: 234 (240 for carry!)
- Life: 295
- 5 Orange Juices (Ver: Crafting#Cooking)
1. ## **Recommended Trades**
Trade your apples to wooden logs, as they'll be worth 8 each in Area 8 instead of 15.

    - Recommended Grinding**

There is nothing special about this area in terms of grinding. Continue hunting to get mermaid hairs, adventuring, working commands, farms and keep training! It is recommended to have at least around 20 mermaid hairs.

1. ## **Recommended Working Commands**
Pickaxe - If you have less than 20m coins and/or tt2 since you get 2 rubies instead of 1. 

Ladder - If you have more than 20m coins.
1. ## **Recommended Gear**
- Sword: Coin Sword (Hyper enchant or above)
- Armor: Coin Armor (Perfect enchant or above)
- Level: 40
- You should also cook 5 Orange Juices (Ver: Crafting#Cooking) to get some extra stats for the dungeon.
