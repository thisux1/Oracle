1. ## **Recommended Grinding**
Area 2 is the area where you unlock your first new commands, and two really strong ones. Enchanting which makes your gear even stronger and training, which is just a nice exp bonus. 

You can't craft stronger gear, but you can now enchant your gear which can get much more powerful, making you take way less damage from hunts and adventures. It's always a good idea to enchant your gear a lot until you can get a good enchantment if you can spare the money, but wasting too much money for a really rare one is not recommended, since the odds are low and it can cost a lot of money early game. Enchant until you get the recommended enchants. Always keep in mind to not waste too much time or coins on it.

Another new command, training is a command you should use over and over again from now on as soon as its cooldown is up. Continue doing your hunts, worker commands and adventures along with it.

Do not forget accepting or rejecting quests (you should not gambe yet), dailies, weeklies, hunt, adventure, and vote every day for tons of loot.

Area 3 is a better area to stay in to grind for resources than this one, and if you have a few million coins, then it's the best in the whole game!
