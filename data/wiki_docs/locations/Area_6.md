1. # Stat Recommendations for Dungeon 6
1. ## **Recommended stats**
- Attack: 214
- Defense: 176 (210 for carry!)
- Life: 245
1. ## **Recommended Grinding**
This and preceding areas is where preparation for edgy sword and armor is continued. 

    - The same as area five except the apples (as they should have been gotten before hand)** 

You should have gotten at least 16,000 apples in Area 5 (If it's your first run) and 30 drops of each mob (Wolf, Zombie and Unicorn). In this Area, you now want to trade all the apples back to logs to gain a HUGE boost on logs, you should craft a Coin Armor now.   

Buy EDGY lootboxes whenever your cooldown is up. Continue hunting, using worker commands, farming and training.  
1. ## **Recommended Working Commands**
Pickaxe - If you have less than 20m coins and/or have 2 Time Travels (you get 2 rubies instead of 1 at tt2; 20m coins is only an indication of which command is more efficient.)

Ladder - If you already have 20m coins or more.

1. ## **Recommended Gear**
- Sword: Coin Sword (Hyper enchant or above)
- Armor: Coin Armor (Ultimate enchant or above)
- Level: 30
1. ## **Recommended Trades**
Category:Areas

In this area, and the next, apples should be traded to wooden logs as they'll temporarily lose their worth in area 8. It is not suggested to sell apples from this point onwards simply because the material loss is way too big. 

Bank should've already been bought by this time, to get a nice little exp bonus.
