<p webmemes_parsed="1">**Area 3** is the best area in Epic RPG. It has the best trading route in the entire game making it the best place to farm materials for your run, but this place in not for everyone. It is only recommended to farm in this area if you have Time Traveled (Ver: Time Travel) at least once,or have enough coins (a few million) to be able to buy edgy lootboxes every time the cooldown resets. Only from Time Travel 5 to above it is recommended to fully farm in this area, before that, spend a bit of your time here and put your main focus in Area 5 and now we'll see an overview about why.

1. # Stat Recommendations for D3 (A3 > D3 > A4)

1. ##  **Recommended stats** 

- Attack: 93
- Defense: 91 (85 for carry! [Yes, you can carry with the normal recommended gear!])
- Life: 170

1. ##  **Recommended Grinding** 
In A3, the recommended materials to grind heavily depend on your amount of Time Travels. 

For players who have only traveled once to four times (TT1 - TT4), they would only be required to farm 20 zombie eyes and 20 wolf skins. **There is no recommended amount of fish for these players.** 

Things start to get interesting from the fifth time travel (TT5+), where now a recommendation of fish amounts is placed by. They would want to farm more in this area because it's faster - since it has the best trade rates - and now, as a time traveler, have many features that for example, a non-time traveler wouldn't have. These include pets, better worker profession (Ver: Professions) levels, more materials per working command, money to constantly buy EDGY lootboxes, etc.

1. ## **Recommended Fish and/or Mob Drops per Time Travel**

- TT1-TT4: 20 zombie eyes and 20 wolf skins.
- TT5-TT9: 60k fish
- TT10-TT24: 80k fish
- TT25+: 400k fish

For TT5+ and above, since they will fully farm in A3, it's recommended to farm 30 wolf skins and 30 zombie eyes in here, instead of 20 for prior time travels. It is recommended to keep buying EDGY lootboxes, but it is **ONLY RECOMMENDED** to open EDGY lootboxes in Area 5, because of the ruby drops.

1. ##  **Recommended Gear** 

- Sword: Zombie Sword - Epic Enchant or better.
- Armor: Epic Armor - Epic Enchant or better.
- Level: 15 or higher.

1. ##  **Recommended Trades** 
In this area, it is recommended to trade **everything** to fish; this is because, from A3 to A4, fish has a multiplier of 2x, meaning you can multiply your materials by 2 just by trading everything to fish in A3. This outweighs every dismantle in the game, because there are more trade rates ahead. Even the ultra log which take 250,000 logs to craft but only gives 81920 when dismantled is a net profit in the long run. Multiplying by 2 may not be an instant profit, but there are more trade rates ahead.
