1. #  Guide for your First Time Travel 
<p webmemes_parsed="1">This is it, Area 11. The area where new players will acquire their first Time Travel. Once you've come here for the first time, you should follow this guide: first, dismantle everything and trade all materials to wooden logs. If you don't have 30 million coins, trade some to apples and sell them until you have 50m, if you have a Tier VI or higher horse, you only need 30 million coins. After that keep crafting epic logs in batches of 500/1000 and dismantle them, repeat this until you get Crafter (Ver: Professions#Crafter) to level 100 or you run out of wooden logs. Lastly, sell all your equipment as it's lost after the Time Travel, your crops, life potions and seeds.
----<p webmemes_parsed="1">**TT1 and above**<p webmemes_parsed="1">Hunts and adventures will now be much harder. It will be hard to go alone, forge some gear.<p webmemes_parsed="1">Quick Guide: Reach level 70, Forge ULTRA-EDGY Sword and enchant to Hyper or higher, Trade before leaving (see trades below)<p webmemes_parsed="1">For Dungeon 11, the player needs a tremendous amount of health, it is recommended at this point to start using your materials to cook Fruit Salads (Ver: Crafting#Cooking), or Baked Fish (Ver: Crafting#Cooking) if you have Epic Fish remaining (Do not craft Epic Fish, if you don't have any Epic Fishes, cook fruit salads instead) Until you have at least 681 HP. 
1. ## **Recommended Trades**
Trade your Rubies  to Logs 

The recommended Working Command (Ver: Working Commands) is Chainsaw, or Drill if you have less than 30 million gold.
