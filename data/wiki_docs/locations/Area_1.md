<p webmemes_parsed="1">Welcome to Epic RPG! This is your starting point to your legendary journey, there are scary wolves, sticky slimes and many other monsters. Your first priority is to level up, and get some quick gold so start by hunting some monsters down.

1. ##  **Getting some levels and gold**
<p webmemes_parsed="1">Whenever you hunt, you will find a monster and kill it which will give you gold and XP, but be careful! Because hunting takes away your HP, and you might die and lose a level. You can go to the shop (Ver: Shop#Buy) and buy some potions to heal up whenever you need it. Use quest to get some tasks for XP and gold! <p webmemes_parsed="1">Another easy way to obtain gold is through daily, weekly and vote which will give you a lot of initial gold. Use this gold to buy yourself some gear, and a horse. Now you should be stronger to last a few hunts without having to heal.  <p webmemes_parsed="1">Dueling (Ver: Duel) with other players should also be highly considered, given if you win, you're given a good amount of XP and gold. Even if you lose, you don't lose anything so it is a win-win situation. 

1. ##  **Getting some materials** 
One of the most key concepts in Epic RPG is the obtaining of resources, such as wood and fish. This is because you need these to craft important gears (Ver: Crafting) to get way stronger, the recommended working command (Ver: Working Commands) in this area is chop. 

A very important thing in this game are horses (Ver: Horse), they give you a lot of buffs that will be very useful especially in the future, so make sure to breed with other horses to tier up and get even stronger buffs!

Once you get enough materials, make sure to craft a Wooden Sword (Ver: Crafting#Swords) and a Fish Armor (Ver: Crafting#Armors), level up to 5 and that's it! You're ready to hop into a dungeon and go to the next area, buy a key in the shop, grab some teammates and fight the dragon!<p webmemes_parsed="1"> <p webmemes_parsed="1">

1. # Recommendations prior dungeon

1. ##  **Recommended stats** 

- Attack: 9
- Defense: 14
- Life: 120

Remember, all teammates need to have at least these or more stats or the dungeon is likely to not succeed!
