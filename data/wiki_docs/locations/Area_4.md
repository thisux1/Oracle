|nextarea=Area 5}}Area 4 is probably one of the most useless Areas, after trading all the fish in A3, you can trade them back to logs in here for a x2 boost in logs. In this Area, there are 2 key commands unlocked, Guilds and Farm (Ver: Farming). 

1. ##  **Guilds** 
Guilds are a core thing for every player to have, not only it gives a huge boost in duel XP and coin rewards, but there are a lot of benefits such as the Guild Shop (Ver: Guilds#Guild%20Shop). To get the currency required, the players in a guild must complete weekly tasks, alongside upgrading their own guild or raiding other guilds! Each Saturday at 22:00 UTC, rewards will be given, being higher rewards for guilds that got most energy. 

1. ##  **Farm** 
Before being able to use farm, you must buy seeds in the shop in order to actually get some crops. Once you use the command, there's a chance that you will either get potato, carrot and bread. Carrot being the most useful for you currently. 

The uses on the crops will vary, but you want to focus on cooking (Ver: Crafting#Cooking) with these items, especially Orange Juices. Orange Juices will boost your ATK and DEF by 3 points each,  which will be very useful for the future. 

-

At this point, you should start buying EDGY lootboxes, trade all items into apples. **All**, so dismantle everything and trade into apples. If you don't have coins, sell 210 apples which should make around 420k, don't worry! You will get a massive profit once you're in Area 5. Make sure to have enough for the key and once you can, get out of Area 4 as soon as you can.

Recommended working command is **Axe.**

1. #  Recommendations Prior to Dungeon 

- Attack: 101
- Defense: 105 
- HP: 195

You should change the enchantment of your EPIC Armor to Hyper, if you haven't done it in Area 3.
