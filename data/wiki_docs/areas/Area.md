# Area

1. # **Description**
An area is what determines a player progress, a higher area means more mobs available, better loot, gears and new upgraded commands. To advance from an area to a higher one, the player must complete a dungeon, and when the player reaches determined area, they must Time Travel in order to keep advancing. Each area has an unique set of mobs, new commands and new features which will be discussed in depth.

1. # **Areas**
There are currently 20 areas in the game, and 2 special ones. The first 11 areas are accessible for all players, from areas 11+ you'll need a certain amount of time travels.

1. # Time Travel**+ Areas.**
After your first time travel, all areas will now have a Time Travel pre-requisite to be able to attempt the dungeon of a certain area.
| Area | New Commands | Requirement | Mobs | Adventure Mobs | Dungeon |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Area 12 | dynamite, pets, ultraining, badge | Time Travel 1+ | Kid Dragon | Hyper Giant Dragon | Dungeon 12 |
| Area 13 | transmute, hunt hardmode | Time Travel 3+ | Teen Dragon | Ancientest Dragon | Dungeon 13 |
| Area 14 | adventure hardmode, big dice | Time Travel 5+ | Adult Dragon | Just Purple Dragon | Dungeon 14 |
| Area 15 | transcend, time jump (Ver: Time Travel#Super%20Time%20Travel) | Time Travel 10+ | Old Dragon | I have no more ideas dragon | Dungeon 15.1 |


1. #  **Special Areas** 
There are 2 Special Areas in the game. The TOP area and Christmas Area 0. They offer special features.
| Area | Requirements | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TOP (Ver: The TOP) | Defeat Dungeon 15.2 | TOP is a special area where the player can confront the Epic NPC, and from there access Area 16+. |
| Area 0 (Ver: Christmas#Christmas area (Area 0)) | Consume a Gingerbread during the Christmas event. | Area 0 is an area where each hunt will give no XP or coins, but it will give 2 Christmas related items. The mob drop is based on max area. |
