# Bank

The bank can be unlocked by spending two EPIC coins (Ver: Epic coins) in the epic-shop (Ver: Shop#EPIC Shop), using the command **/buy bank account**. 

1. #  Benefit 
The bank grants a small XP bonus in hunt and adventure based on the amount that you have deposited into it. You may deposit (Ver: Bank#Deposit) or withdraw (Ver: Bank#Withdraw) coins from the bank at any time.

Using the command **/bank** displays the amount of coins deposited as well the XP bonus granted from the amount deposited.

Roughly, the formula for bonus xp is the following:  

XP bonus = **1+0,6*Log10(coins)**      (this formula does not apply with coins = 0)  

1. #  Upgraded epic rpg bank
If you have a  **coin ring**, your bank will  upgrade. It will give a lot higher XP bonus and if you have a T10 horse it will also increase your chance of getting x2 monster drops in <code>hunt</code> and <code>adventure</code>. 
 

1. # Deposit
Depositing coins in the bank will provide a bonus of XP, but the coins cannot be used until withdrawn.

Usage: **/deposit [amount of coins]**

The **amount of coins** can be a number, "all" or "half".

1. # Withdraw
Withdrawing coins from the bank will reduce the bonus of XP obtained, but make the coins available to use.

Usage: **/withdraw [amount of coins]**

The **amount of coins** can be a number, "all" or "half".
