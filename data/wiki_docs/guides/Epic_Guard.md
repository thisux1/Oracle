# Epic Guard

Epic Guard is a captcha system to make sure players are not cheating in the game and determine which users have been violating the rules of EPIC RPG. It's triggered randomly and you won't be able to type any /command until you either do the captcha or until the time runs out. The captcha consists of an image that shows a certain item from the game, you must name the item correctly before the timer runs out to continue playing. When the time runs out, or if you answer the captcha incorrectly 3 times, you'll be put in jail and you won't be able to do any /command except for **/jail**, when you use **/jail** you get two options and you simply have to type those words to trigger action:

- **Protest**: Upon writing this, you would get another captcha and you must answer it correctly to be able to use commands again, otherwise you'll be put back in jail again.

- **kill:** You would attempt to kill the guard but there is a 70% chance to fail and lose XP. If your attempt to kill the epic guard is successful, you will gain certain amount of XP. However, you will still remain in jail as another epic guard will replace the killed one. 

1. ##   Different captchas 
Captchas have many variants in order to counteract attempts at cheating.

The captcha could:

- be the original image of an item in the game
- be stretched or compressed
- be grayscaled (turned into a gray/dark-grayish color)
- contain random lines
- be any combination of the previous modifiers

Note: You won't get specific captchas if you haven't unlocked that item yet in your area, for example you won't get the dragon scale captcha until you reach A11+.
