# Cooldowns

Cooldowns are what decide the amount of time it takes for you to be able to do that command again and get rewards. In EPIC RPG, cooldowns can range from 1 minute upwards to 7 days.

When you use **/cd** or **@EPIC RPG#4117 cooldowns** it will show exactly how many days, hours, minutes, and/or seconds it will take before your commands come back up to use again.

Cooldowns can be reduced by becoming a donator on Patreon, reductions are 10%, 20% and 35%.

Some events also have cooldown reductions for all commands or for some commands specifically.

1. #  Commands 

- **/cd** or **@EPIC RPG#4117 cds** : Displays the player's cooldowns.
- **/rd** or **@EPIC RPG#4117 rd**: Displays the player's cooldowns that are ready.
      - /cd max** or **@EPIC RPG#4117 cd max**: Displays the player's cooldowns but will show the highest available tier commands instead.
      - /rd max** or **@EPIC RPG#4117 rd max**: Displays the player's cooldowns that are ready but will show the highest available tier commands instead.
The cooldowns that aren't included in this section are the EPIC shop consumables (Ver: Shop#Consumables) items, guild (Ver: Guilds) cooldown and pet adventure (Ver: Pets) cooldown.
