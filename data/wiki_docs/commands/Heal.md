# Heal

Use this command to heal yourself when your HP is low.

If you do not heal, you will eventually die and lose 1 level. The exception to this is if you have a tier IV horse or better, in which case you will not lose a level but instead you will drop to 1hp and not receive any rewards.

To buy Life Potions, use **/buy life potion [amount].** Life Potions are also obtainable via **/daily** and **/wheel**. The amount of Life Potions obtained from **/daily** is based on your max area.

Life Potions can be bought in the shop for 25 coins each, and can also be sold for 20 coins regardless of the level of your Merchant Profession (Ver: Professions).

You also cannot heal if you are at max HP (or have a life boost activated).

If you will try to heal in A16, you will lose 1% of your max HP. to avoid it, you can brew a potion potion (Ver: Alchemy) or keep purchasing life boosts.

Alternatively, you can heal yourself by consuming the bread crop. See Farming -> Crops for more info.

    - Note**: Healing has a chance of activating the heal event. More information on this random event can be found in the Events page.<gallery>
