With this command players can choose a title. Players can see the IDs of titles they have unlocked with **/title list**.

1. # **Badges**
Command: **/badge select [ID]** 

This command is used for both equipping and unequipping. You can see your unlocked badges with **/badge unlocked**.

Players can select up to three badges to display on their profile (5 with Tier X horse). Most badges can be obtained from reaching a certain amount of coolness points. Points are earned from ultraining (Ver: Training#Ultraining) and can by achieved by epic results while playing i.e. forging an omega sword, opening a GODLY lootbox, dropping Ultra logs, etc.. A list of badges can be seen via the command: **/badge list 1-3**.
