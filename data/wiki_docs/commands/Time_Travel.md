# Time Travel

- Time travel** (commonly referred to as TT) is the ability to reset the level, stats, equipment, etc. of the player's profile, sending them back to the start in exchange for permanent boosts that make the next run faster. The player will also be granted access to four more enchant possibilities (see: Enchant) after their first (OMEGA), third (ULTRA-OMEGA), fifth (GODLY) and fifteenth (VOID) reset. 

    - Warning**: Please keep in mind that the area you will be able to use the time travel command changes based on the amount of time travels you have! You can always check **/time travel** to see if and when you can time travel, the command is always accessible once you reach a11 for the first time and even after you time travel.

1. #  **What Gets Reset and What Stays** 
The player will be brought back to the start, meaning that they will be brought back to level 1 with no equipment, lose most items in their inventory and will be placed back in area 1. That means that the player should use their materials into something such as professions or trading them into apples, and selling these apples since they yield more coins than any other materials. Since the equipment is also reset, the player should always sell their equipment before time travelling. 

After the 2nd time travel, you cannot give coins to users in TT0 or TT1, and neither can receive coins from them. After the 20th time travel, you cannot give coins to players in TT0 to TT19 and as same with TT2 restriction, you can't neither receive coins from them. 

Note: If you bought a stat boost from an event shop, the stat buff will stay even after the event is over, however, it will be lost when you time travel. 

Although most items are lost (as stated above), here are some of the things that are carried over with you after time traveling: 
- Horse - they will stay the same as before you time traveled
- Coins - bank included; refers to epic and guild coins as well
- Arena Cookies
- Any items purchased from the Epic Shop
- Professions
- Pets
- Event items (If there is one active)
- Dragon essences (Not to be confused with Dragon scales, a mob loot dropped in Area 11-15)
- Time Dragon Essences 

1. #  **Time Travel Boost** 
The player is able to time travel multiple times so long as they reach the required area. The max number of regular time travels is 25; however there is no cap once the player unlocks Super Time Travel.

Time travel count also increases the chance to advance a tier in **horse breeding** and **pet fusion**.

(49 + x) * x / 2

(99 + x) * x / 2

e.g. for TT5 : (99 + 5) * 5 / 2 = 260

(99 + x) * x / 4

1. #  **Titles** 
Upon reaching some milestones in the amount of time travels, certain titles will be unlocked for the player:
- "Time traveler" - unlocked after the first reset
- "One time wasn't enough" - unlocked once the second reset is achieved
- "I spend too much time here" - unlocked after the 5th reset
- "OOF" - unlocked once the 10th reset is reached
- "OOFMEGA" - unlocked once the 25th reset is achieved
- "GOOFDLY" - unlocked once the 50th reset is achieved 
- "VOOFID" - unlocked once the 75th reset is achieved 

1. #  **Super Time Travel** 
Unlocked once the player defeats Dungeon 15 and obtains the TIME KEY, Super Time Travel (or STT for short) is a higher tier command of Time Travel.

Before Super Time Traveling, players will be prompted to choose a single, mandatory reward that will help aid in their next run. These rewards are purchased based on points (Time Travel Score). 
The player does not sell their equipment and inventory for coins, but rather has to (partially) "sacrifice" the items (have them in inventory at the time of STT) to increase that score. Keeping (not selling) the equipment (sword and armor) alone gives ~355.5 points. The best trade if striving for points is rubies.
