# Trade

EPIC RPG's trading system allows you to exchange a number of base resources (wooden log, normie fish, apple, and ruby) to other base resources in a set rate/ratio depending on your **max area**, as long as you have unlocked the area in which these resources become available.

1. # **Commands**

    - /trade**
: Lists out your current tradeable resources, trade rates, and the ID letter for trading.

    - /trade [ID] (amount)**

: Conducts the trade; replace the [ID] with the letter of your choice, and (amount) with the number of times you want to do this trade.
NOTE: The trade will only be one of the item you are trading to if you do not input an amount.

: ex) [ /trade b 40 ] or [ /trade d all ]

Upon entering a command, the exchange will be immediately carried without a confirmation. There will be an embed showing the items traded.

1. # **Trade Rates**

    - The trade rates change based on your max area** and are set as such due to balancing the work commands so as the yield would be similar to what you would get from a set number of work commands.

The key element of trade rates is ratio — the trade ratio between the player's current max area and the next reachable max area. If followed correctly, they can help save a big chunk of time which players mostly spend on grinding.

Example: Wooden log to apple trade ratio in area 5 is 4:1, compared with wooden log to apple trade ratio in area 6 which is 15:1. Apple to apple trade ratio between area 6 and area 5 is 15:4 which is 3.75. If the player trades all their logs with apples while in Area 5, they can gain a significant amount of profit after reaching Area 6 and reverting the apples back into logs. Thus, the player gains 3.75 x [initial amount of wooden logs].

    - If you do your trades correctly prior to doing each Dungeon and moving to the next area, 1,000,000 A3 logs can become 16,875,000 A10 logs.**
