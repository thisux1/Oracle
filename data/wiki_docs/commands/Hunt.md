# Hunt

This is the /hunt command, the most basic command to gain XP, coins, mob drops (Ver: Drops) and sometimes even lootboxes (Ver: Lootbox) or epic berries (Ver: Epicness)!

If you die while doing a hunt, you will lose 1 level as well as any experience gained; however if you have a tier IV horse (or better), instead of dying and losing a level, your horse will save you and you will stay at 1 HP. Even if you then do hunt again at 1 HP, your horse will still save you, but you also will not get any rewards for failed hunts.

So as long as you do not have a tier IV horse, always make sure to hea!. Life Potions are very cheap, costing only 25 coins each. So better be safe and heal more often. Even if you have a tier IV horse, being saved by your horse does not give you rewards so you should still heal beforehand to ensure you get the drops.

The damage you receive from hunt is reduced by your attack and defense stats. One more point in one of those stats means one less damage taken. Thus, it is possible to take 0 damage from hunt if your stats are high enough.

1. #  Hunt Command Variations 
There are three variations of the basic hunt command: **/hunt hardmode**,** /hunt alone**,** and /hunt together**.

- **/hunt hardmode -**  unlocked at Area 13 and gives a ton of experience, coins, a higher chance to get monster drops, and a better chance to get a higher tier lootbox. Be warned, however, the damage taken compared to normal hunts is incredibly high.
- **/hunt alone** **-** an added command in which the player will face an overpowered mob based on the area the player is in without the aid of the player's horse. The purpose of this command is to die and lose a level intentionally, so your horse will not save you even if it is Tier IV or higher. Even if you survive in hunt alone, you will receive the same reward as a regular hunt and will not gain any higher rewards. You must be level 100 or higher to be able to use this command.
- **/hunt together -** a command reserved only for married players. This command runs the hunt command for both players, therefore using up both hunt cooldowns. If the player issuing the hunt together command has another variation of hunt available (i.e. hunt hardmode and/or hunt alone), they may issue it and add "together" at the end of the command to also run the hunt for their partner.

> Ex) /hunt hardmode together - /hunt alone together

Notes:

- The partner of the initiator will always have a normal hunt done whether or not they have the other variation(s) of hunt unlocked.
- There are 2 designs for hunt together, new and old (shortcuts being "n" and "o" respectively). Default design for slash commands is the new one, and the default design for normal commands is the old one.


1. # Mob Drops
Has a 3.5% chance of getting a drop when you hunt, or 7% when you find the mob that drops it. Dark energy is different by having a chance drop chance of 0.1% but can be dropped from all hunt mob. The chance increases as you time travel (Ver: Time Travel) and in higher Horse tiers.

Note: For a1-15 mob drop, the chance stated in their help section are 4%, this is because the chance is buffed in the 2025 QOL update however **rpg help** was not updated to reflect that change.

The drop that you obtain from hunt depends on your area:

In addition, there is a chance to get a lootbox (Ver: Shop#Lootbox). Most lootboxes will be common and uncommon, and GODLY lootboxes are extremely rare. If you get a lootbox, open it with **/open**.
