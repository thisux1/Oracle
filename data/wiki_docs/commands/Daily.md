# Daily

The Daily command allows the player to claim a certain amount of coins and life potions (Ver: Heal). The amount of coins and life potions depend on the player's max area. Daily rewards can be claimed every **23 hours and 50 minutes**. After a 7+ Daily Streak, the player will also receive an Epic Coin and a Flask.

The coins received are affected by the horse tier.
