# Drops

Here you will find information about the different drops for hunting (Ver: Hunt) and working commands.


1. #  **Combat** 

1. ## **Hunt**
Mob drops can be mainly obtained from hunting or from lootboxes, they are used for crafting equipment. 

The base chance of getting a drop after a new update is 7% per hunt. Mob drops chances can be increased by time travelling (Ver: Time Travel), a tier VII or better horse (Ver: Horse#Horse%20Tier%20Boosts), hardmode version of hunt, through a world boost (Ver: World Boost) or through potions (Ver: Alchemy).

Note: If you have dragon scales in inventory, instead of forging you also have an option to sell them for coolness.

Higher tiers of chopping commands give more logs in quantity except for HYPER log and above, although the chances are unaffected. SUPER logs and above are locked by area. For example, it's not possible to get HYPER log from chopping commands in Area 2. To see more in depth about working commands, see Working Commands (Ver: Working Commands#Chop).

1. ## **Mine**
The 4 mining commands available to the player, *mine, pickaxe, drill and dynamite* work differently than previous one. This time, higher tier of commands do not mean more rubies, but more coins and a better chance for rubies! The first tier of command, mine, is unlocked at Area 5 alongside with the ability to trade rubies with logs and vice versa. Player's max area significantly affect the amount of coins gathered per command, but there is also some luck involved. The coin drop has 70% to 130% range so the amount will also depend on your luck.
