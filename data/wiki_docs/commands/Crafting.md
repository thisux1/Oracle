# Crafting

Crafting is an essential feature of the game. Being used to craft a variety of equipment that can make you stronger, to crafting higher tier of materials with a lower one. Keep in mind that dismantling higher tiers of a material into lower tiers will only give back 80% of the materials required to craft it.

Your Crafter (Ver: Professions#Crafter) Profession plays a vital role when crafting items, as there is a chance of getting back 12.25% of the items used to craft the item. The chance depends on your crafter profession level, being a higher chance with a higher crafter profession level.

Having a crafter profession with a higher level than 100 allows you to get more than 10% back. The formula to calculate the percentage return with each level is
> 12.5 + (225 * (Crafter pr LVL - 100))^0.2


1. # Crafting Swords and Armors
The recipes for swords and armors can be viewed with **/recipes** or **/recipes 2**

Before crafting an equipment, the players needs to meet certain requirements to craft one. These are: Have enough levels, materials and don't have any equipment equipped.

1. # Converting Items (Craft/Dismantle) 
Command: **/craft/dismantle [item] [amount]**  

The command **craft** converts lower tier materials into higher tier materials.  

And the command **dismantle** converts higher tier materials into lower tier materials but with a 20% loss of the materials it takes to craft that item.  

Item recipes are accessible with **rpg recipes 4 **or** rpg recipes items**.

It takes 250,000 logs to craft 1 ultra log, however 1 ultra log can only be dismantled into 81,920 logs (67.23% loss).

1. #  Cooking 
Recipes are accessible with **/recipes 5** or **@EPIC RPG#4117** **recipes cook**

​​​​​​​​​​​​​​You can cook items by typing **/cook [item] [amount]**

Stat buffs through cooking and level boosts are permanent until you use the Time Travel command, upon which the boosts vanish. Once an item is cooked, you will automatically gain the stat buff instantly.

Profession boosts are permanent even after time travel, as professions do not reset upon time traveling.

    - NOTE**: The item is consumed when cooked, so it cannot be kept in the inventory.


1. # Forging
The command **/forge** is unlocked in Area 11 which allows the player to upgrade their Edgy gear even better gears. Unlike crafting, forging requires you to have the previous tier of equipment to forge the next one. Also, enchants stay instead of being lost like when crafting normally. Forging is required to be able to enter Dungeons 11 and higher. **rpg start advanced** will show the requirements.
