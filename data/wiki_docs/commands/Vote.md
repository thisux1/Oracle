# Vote

You can vote for the **Epic RPG** discord bot and receive rewards such as coins, items, and reset cooldowns for specific commands.

    - Note:** You can only vote every 12 hours. Trying to vote again before the 12 hours are up will not grant rewards.

1. # **Commands**
- **/vote**
    - Shows you what rewards you can earn, what your current streak is, the link to vote, and the cooldown.

1. # **Vote Streak**
The more you vote in a row, the higher your streak will be. The maximum streak you can currently have is 7.

1. # **Vote Rewards**
- The amount of coins you get depends on your **level** and **streak**.
- You are guaranteed **25**  **Arena Cookies** and a Flask once you reach a **streak** of **7**.
- You are guaranteed **1**  **Epic Coin**.
- You are guaranteed an **Adventure Cooldown Reset**.
    - You can get a **Miniboss / Dungeon Cooldown Reset** on Fridays, Saturdays, or Sundays (UTC).
- The type of Lootbox you get depends on your **streak**. The highest type of Lootbox you can get is an **EDGY Lootbox**.
| Streak | Lootbox |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Rare Lootbox |
| 1 | Epic Lootbox |
| 2 - 7 | Edgy Lootbox |


1. # **Trivia**

- You used to get **Life Potions**, but they were changed to **Lootboxes** during the **2021 Easter Event**, then changed back. Now **Life Potions** are removed from **Vote** rewards.
- There was a hidden streak counter that would eventually lead to **Epic Coins** being a reward. Now you can see the counter and **Epic Coins** are guaranteed.
- There used to not be a visible cooldown in the **Cooldown** command.
