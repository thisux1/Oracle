# Duel

Request a duel against another player. Players will have 3 randomised options to choose from, out of a total of 7 different weapons. You will have the same options as the other. 

Declining a duel will not consume your cooldowns. Accepting a duel but not responding with an option will automatically do 1 damage. If both players do not respond within 30 seconds, the duel will cancel. Players can deal the same damage, resulting in a tie with no rewards.

The rewards are based on the player with the lower level. Rewards can be affected by the winner's time travel bonus, patreon level, and guild level. Both players can earn Guild XP (Ver: Guilds); awarded by duelling players outside your guild and more with a similar level player.

    - Duel Rewards:** 

Experience: [RNG] * [1 + BOOSTS] * [1 + GUILD BONUS] * [1 + TT BONUS] * [1,2] * 7.9 * LEVEL^2 

RNG for experience is 0.8-1.2.

Coins: [RNG] * [1 + BOOSTS] * [1 + GUILD BONUS] * [1,2] * 21.25 * LEVEL^2 

RNG for coins is 0.94-1.06.

For the [1,2] section you have to choose between 1 if you're not a Donator in Patreon, 2 if you are (Patreon perks provide x2 duel rewards).

      - Note:** A player is able to obtain lootboxes from duels (EDGY ~ Common) if a Guild (Ver: Guilds) Lootbooxst is active. But the guild coins required to buy this item aren't obtainable anymore.*

The 7 different weapons and the stats they are based on are:

- **Dagger **is based on **Attack Stat**
- **Shield** is based on **Defense Stat**
- **Anchor** is based on **Max HP** (life)
- **Ruler** is based on **Player's Level**
- **Credit Card** is based on **Coins** (in total)
- **Tools** is based on **Player's Gear** (both sword and armor; excluding enchantments)
- **Magnet** is based on the **Enchantment** on both sword and armor

    - Duel Damage Formula** = (Stat corresponding to the weapon) * (Logarithmic Weapon multiplier)^[1] * (Random Multiplier)^[2]

^[1]The weapon multiplier is logarithmic, making it unknown and almost impossible to estimate.

^[2]The randomness factor applied is approximately ±25% of the chosen stat.
