# Enchant

The **enchant** command is used to add an enchantment to your sword or armor. 

Enchantments increase the stats of your sword/armor. There are a total of **14 possible enchantments**.

The enchant you get is completely random and will replace your existing enchant, which means there is the possibility of downgrading your enchant.

After you reach level 101+ in your enchanter profession (Ver: Professions#Enchantments), you will have a chance to earn money in an enchant instead of losing it.

While enchanting, you have a chance to drop **Master key part C** . You have a higher chance of dropping it if you are using a better enchanting command or if you have a higher time travel. For more information, see artifacts.

- Sword or armor can appear to be "broken" while enchanting. Note that this is only a hidden event that can trigger and your sword/armor **will not actually break**. See events for more information.*

1. # Usage
The command usage is: **rpg** or **/[enchant/refine/transmute/transcend] [sword/armor]**

Examples: **rpg enchant armor**, **/refine sword**

1. # Command Tiers 
Higher tiers of the enchant command are unlocked in certain areas. These commands have a multiplier, which multiplies the cost of the enchantment and the chances of getting a better enchant. 
| Command | Area required | Enchanting multiplier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enchant | 2 | 1 |
| Refine | 7 | 10 |
| Transmute | 13 | 100 |
| Transcend | 15 | 1000 |


    - The average result is equivalent for all commands**, so it's better to use the highest tier available as less commands have to be used to achieve the desired enchant.

The cost is calculated by the following formula:

Coins = [max area] * [multiplier of enchanting command] * [time travel enchanting multiplier] 

1. # Enchant Boosts
The Sword/Armor enchantment will improve your overall ATK/DEF stats, roughly based on these formulas: 

- ATK = (level + cooked stats + sword attack + event stats + potions stats) * (100% + horse bonus as %) * (100% + **enchantment bonus as %**)
- DEF = (level + cooked stats + armor defense + event stats + potions stats) * (100%+ horse bonus as %) * (100% + **enchantment bonus as %**)
If you have a Magic horse, the formula will be the following: 

- ATK = (level + cooked stats + sword attack + event stats + potions stats) * (100% + (**enchant buff%** * (100% + magic buff%)))
    - i.e. An ETERNAL enchant value of 305% with a MAGIC EFFICIENCY of .8316 would result in a total multiplier of 6.58638 on base stats because 1 + 3.05 * (1+.8316) = 6.58638
- DEF = (level + cooked stats + armor defense + event stats + potions stats) * (100% + (**enchant buff%** * (100% + magic buff%)))

Horse bonus refers to the horse boost percentage shown in "/horse". Enchantment bonus refers to the enchantment boost percentage shown in "/help enchant" or in the table above.

1. # Possible Boosts
When you have a tier 8 horse or above, your chance for better enchantments are increased **greatly**. 

There is a time travel multiplier, which increases both price and efficiency for better enchantments depending on your time travel. You can see this boost with **/time travel.** 

Enchanter profession increases the chance for better enchantments (see below) but the boost is unnoticeable. 

There are **3 alchemy recipes** (see rpg recipes alchemy and rpg alchemy info ) that can be used to boost your enchant chances. 
> The potions that buff enchant luck are the following:


- These potions are:
- the **inverted potion :** 11h, 11m, 11s of +15% Enchant luck, +40% Random Events
- the **liquid hair potion:** 5h of +5% Enchant luck, and -10% Pets adventure time.
- the **void potion:** 1d and 12h of +300% Enchant luck, +2 Lootbox drops rarity, and +50% Monster drops.

1. # Enchanter Profession XP
Refer to enchanter profession (Ver: Professions#Enchantments) for information about enchanter profession XP earned through the enchant command and its upgrades.

1. #  Ascended players 
Ascended players can use higher tier enchanting commands if they have unlocked it previously. The cost does not change and still follows the formulas in the table above. This way, players can gain access to enchanting in area 1.
