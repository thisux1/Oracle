# Weekly

The weekly command allows the player to claim a certain amount of coins along with 1  lootbox, 1 to 3  time cookies and 2Flasks. The amount of coins and tier of the lootbox depend on the player's max area. Weekly rewards can be claimed every **7 days**.

While claiming weekly, if you're TT1+ there is a chance of getting a **Pocket watch part D**. For more information about it, see artifacts.

The coins received are affected by the horse tier.
