# Quest

1. #  Description 
The Quest command allows you to pick up a rpg-style quest every 6 hours which is based on your max area. You can decline the quest offered to you, in which case there is a 1 hour cooldown before you can check for a new quest.

1. #  Usage 
- /quest start / @EPIC RPG#4117 quest
    - Pick up a quest if you do not have one yet
    - Shows your current quest and how close you are to finishing it,  if you have one that is not completed
    - Gives you the reward for completing your current quest, if the objective was reached
- /quest quit
    - Cancels your current quest

- /epic quest
    - Lets you enter an epic quest

    - WARNING:** Clearing a dungeon after accepting a quest but prior to completing said quest will automatically cancel any existing quest that you have accepted.

1. #  Quest Types 
- Hunt Quest: kill 3 <mob> (specific enemy based on your highest Area)
    - This requires you to kill 3 of a specific mob that appear while hunting (Ver: Hunt) in your highest unlocked area.

- Adventure Quest: kill 1 <mob> (specific enemy based on your highest Area)
    - This requires you to kill 1 of a specific mob that appears while adventuring (Ver: Adventure) in your highest unlocked area.

- Craft (Ver: Crafting) <quantity> <material> (both based on your highest Area)
    - This requires you to craft the specified amount of the specified material, either by crafting from lower tier materials or dismantling higher tier materials.
- Gambling Quest: win (# of Coins) or higher from any gambling command.
    - This requires you to use any gambling command to win the specified amount of money (e.g. Slots, Blackjack, Coinflip, etc.)
- Join an Arena
    - This requires you to join any arena, you don't need to be the person to start it, you can do so by joining someone else's arena.
- Summon and Kill a Miniboss
    - This requires you to use your miniboss cooldown to summon a miniboss, and kill it. If the miniboss is not defeated, the quest will not be completed.

- Cooking Quest
    - Unlocked in area 5 or above, you need to cook certain food stated in the quest in order to complete the quest. Cooking quest give more exp and less coins than normal quest rewards.

- Guild Quest
    - Unlocked in area 4 or above, in this quest you need to start a guild raid.

- Trading Quest
    - Requires you to trade once with the EPIC NPC.

1. #  Rewards 
Completing a quest gives you coins and experience based on your level.

The rewards of a quest do not auto-claim; you must use **/quest start/progress** after completing the quest to claim its rewards.

Each Quest gives XP and Coins depending on your level and with a new update each quest type gives an extra reward depending on what the quest is about. Amount of items given varies on max area and level.

-Epic Lootboxes  for The Gambling Quest

-Uncommon Lootboxes  for Hunt "X" Mob 3 times Quest

-Wooden Logs  for Adventure Quest (Kill X Mob 1 times in Adventures)

-Epic Logs  for Summoning and **Successfully** **Killing** a Miniboss Quest

-Normie Fish  for Crafting X Number of item Quest

-Golden Fish  for Doing a Trade with the Epic NPC Quest

-Bananas  for Cooking X item 3 times Quest

-Arena Cookies  for Joining an Arena Quest

-Guild Rings  for Raid/Upgrade Guild Quest

1. #  Epic Quest 
The Epic quest is a completely different type of quest, with it's harsh requirement of having either a Special or Super Special (Ver: Horse#Horse%20Types%20and%20Boosts) horse type. This command allows you to fight between 1 to 100 waves of enemies which progressively get stronger each wave. Though there are 100 waves available for the player to defeat, having a **Special horse type** will only allow you to fight up to 15 waves, instead of 100.   

After starting the epic quest command, the player will be prompted to choose a number of waves to fight against with. Which will give rewards in form of XP and coins depending on the amount of waves defeated, and horse level and tier. After typing the desired amount of waves to fight, the player will fight the chosen number of waves. 

If the player fails to defeat all the waves chosen, it will be saved by the horse to prevent it from dying, regardless of it's tier, though no rewards will be given.

1. ##  **Stats Guide** 
Since each wave gets progressively stronger, the player needs to have high stats to be able to defeat higher waves. But it is never recommended to get your stats higher to be able to defeat higher waves, since the rewards are not worth it. Instead, it is recommended to choose the amount of waves you can take on as your equipment and stats get higher through the course of your run rather than trying to get to a certain wave.
