# Horse

Horses are an EPIC Adventurer's trusty companion who will help you in various ways as you play through EPIC RPG!

Note: You can only have one horse partner in your adventures and **cannot sell them**. Breed horses with another player to essentially reroll your horse.

To start breeding horse's with other players, you must first obtain a horse through **/buy basic horse**.

1. # **Commands**
- **/buy basic horse** 
    - Purchases a **Tier I** horse for 500 coins from the shop. (Ver: Shop) You will get a random type except for special and super special. Alias: **@EPIC RPG#4117** **buy horse**

- **/help horse** 
    - Gives an explanation on Horse Tiers, Breeding, Types and other miscellaneous information.

- **/horse stats** 
    - Shows the current profile of your horse. Contains info on their Tier, Level, Boosts, current failed attempts, and a list of other Horse Commands.

- **/horse breeding @player** 
    - Breeds your horse with the horse of the mentioned player. Alias: **@EPIC RPG#4117** **horse breed @player**

- **/horse race** 
    - Enters your horse for racing, only the top 3 win rewards. Requires a Tier V or above to participate. Races end every 2 hours on a set time regardless of when you join.

- **/horse training [amount]**
    - Levels up your horse at the cost of coins. The price will be shown upon a successful training or by referring to the amount shown in your horse's profile and is affected by the level of your lootboxer profession. Alias: **@EPIC RPG#4117** **horse tr**

- **/horse feed**
    - Gives your horse a carrot (obtainable from farming), changing the name of your horse. 
      - /eat horse**
    - "Regret your words"

1. # **Horse Breeding**

There are 10 Horse Tiers - Tier I, Tier II, Tier III, Tier IV, Tier V, Tier VI, Tier VII, Tier VIII ,Tier IX and Tier X

Breeding two horses together will result in a possible new Horse Type, Tiers, Levels and Epicness.

The resulting **Horse Types** will be totally random and is unaffected by anything. The chance to get a specific horse type is the same for all other horse types, excluding **Special** and **Super Special**. While **Special** may be obtained through breeding at a lower chance than other types, **Super Special** can only be obtained by breeding two **Special** horses with one another.

    - Horse Token:** If you do not want to change your horse type during breeding, you can buy a horse token in epic shop with 4 epic coins. If you have it in your inventory, it will be automatically used during breeding. You can sell it for the same amount of epic coins too.

The new **Horse Level** is dependent on both their parent's levels, resulting in the average level of the two. If the average level results in a decimal, it will either be rounded down or up randomly.

    - [(Horse A Level + Horse B Level) / 2] = Average** 
> **Ex #1)** If Horse A Level = 5, and Horse B Level = 9

Then [(5+9) / 2] = 7 

    - Ex #2)** Horse A = 6 and Horse B = 9

[(6+9) / 2] = 7.5 → (7 or 8)

Thus, either/both new horses can be level 7 or level 8


1. #  Horse tiers 
There are 10 **Horse Tiers**. You can get a higher tier horse by breeding your horse with another player's horse. It is not guaranteed to advance to a higher tier upon breeding (Tier I excluded) and may require multiple attempts at breeding before you succeed.
> Tier V + Tier V = Tier V or Tier VI

There's a horse pity system where at a certain amount of breeds, you will have a guaranteed chance for a tier up. Every certain breed, there will be an increased chance to tier up.

Horse breeding and horse race have a shared cooldown of 24 hours. This cooldown is affected if there is a reduction from a patreon role. 

1. #  Max breeding attempts 
Even though there is a certain chance to tier up in each horse tier, there is also a maximum amount of times it can fail before it becomes guaranteed (see table above).

Not only that, but also after a certain amount of fails, the chance to tier up is boosted:

- **Between 0 - 25%** of the fail count to reach a guaranteed horse tier up, there is **no boost**
- **Between 25 - 50%**, the boost is **x1.25** (For horses tier 8 and above)
- **Between 50 - 75%**, the boost is **x2** (For horses tier 6 and above)
- **Between 75 - 100%**, the boost is **x5** (For horses tier 4 and above)

So, for example, a tier 7 horse with 61 fail attempts has 2% chance to tier up, instead of 1%.

1. # **Horse Race**
Available to Tier V and above horse tiers, races are another way a player may be able to increase their horse tier. Results are posted on the Epic RPG Support Server every 2 hours, displaying the top 3 ranking of each of the six tier races (for a total of at most, 18 players displayed).

Rankings are, for the most part, randomly number generated; however, the player's horse level can increase their chances of placing in the top 3. Do note that **a high level does not always guarantee a top 3 rank**. A max level horse of that tier has the possibility of placing 2nd or 3d, or not at all.

Aside from the chance of tiering up, players may instead receive a different reward that is also based on their placing.

1. # **Horse Training and Levels**

Training your horse increases your horse level, which increases the boost that your horse type provides. In the section below, a chart can be found containing the boost percentage per level for each type.

There is a limit to the amount of times you can raise your level depending on horse tier. Having a lootboxer profession level above 100 affects the limit. The formula below can be used to calculate the max level for your tier:

    - (Horse tier * 10) + (Lootboxer Profession Levels above 100)** 

The cost of a horse training increases depending on the horse's level. Lootboxer profession also decreases the cost. The formula below can be used to calculate the cost of a training: 

    - round((n^4 * (n^2 + 210 * n + 2200) * (500 - l^1.2)/100000))**

    - Note:** Horse level does not affect the chances to tier up from a horse breed in any way. 
1. # **Horse Types and Boosts**

Below are the percentage boost per horse level depending on horse type and tier.

    - Defender** increases your Defense. **Strong** increases your Attack. **Tank** increases your HP. **Special** unlocks and boosts the amount of Coins and Exp you gain from Epic Quest (Ver: Quest#Epic Quest). **Golden** boosts the amount of coins you gain from Hunts (Ver: Hunt) and Adventures (Ver: Adventure). **Festive** increases your chances for Random events (Ver: Regular Events#Random Events). **Magic** increases efficiency for enchantments, and Super Special has same effect as Special but unlocks 100 waves!

    - Note: for the Magic Horse Type, efficiency for enchantments does not mean you get a chance for better enchantments. Instead it boosts the % on the attack/defense of an enchantment!**
