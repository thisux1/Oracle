# Farming

The **farm** command is used to get **crops**, which have a variety of purposes. Farming also grants XP.

The **farm** command is unlocked in area #4.


1. #  Seeds 

Farming can be done using the **/farm** command. It requires one seedin the player's inventory. Players can hold at most 10,000 seeds at a time. 

    - Seed drops**

| Seed | Crops obtained ^[1] | Seed drop chance | Seeds obtained |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| normal seed | 2 - 3 (any) | 4% | 4 - 5 (any) |
| potato seed | 5 - 7 (potato) | 75% | 1 - 2 (potato seed) |
| carrot seed | 5 - 7 (carrot) | 75% | 1 - 2 (carrot seed) |
| bread seed | 5 - 7 (bread) | 75% | 1 - 2 (bread seed) |

[1] this is the base drop; actual quantities are affected by time travels (Ver: Time Travel) and possibly potions

Planting a seed will grant crops, including a chance seeds of said crop. It is possible to plant specific seeds with **/farm [seed type]**. Crop specific seeds are guaranteed to drop their crop type (e.g. carrot seeds will always drop carrots). You can also buy special seeds in the guild (Ver: Guilds) shop.

1. ##  Failed seed event 
There is a hidden event in which a seed will not grow after planting it. For more information, see regular events (Ver: Regular Events).

1. #  Crops 
Crops can be used in various crafting and cooking recipes (Ver: Crafting), which can be seen using the commands **/recipes**, **/recipes advanced**, and **/recipes cook.** 

    - Carrots** can be fed to a horse through **/horse feed** in order to reroll its name. 

    - Bread** can be used to heal using **/heal bread**, and using bread to heal also gives a +5 life boost above max HP (This boost is not stackable). It also gives more STT score.

    - Potatoes** can be used to cook carrotato chips for profession (Ver: Professions) XP.
