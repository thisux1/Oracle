# Valentine

Valentine's event starts on the second week of February and last for 7 day, it is a short event but introduces new commands, items, a new mob and fun mechanics to interact with your partner. 

This event gives a 25% cooldown reduction for the hunt command cooldown, and a 10% cooldown reduction for the adventure command if the player is married (Ver: Marry).

    - A few notes and tips about this event:**

- The base chance to find a pink wolf in <code>hunt</code> is 0.5%. This chance increases to 1% when world boost is active. You can check it with <code>rpg world</code>.
- You can get 4-12 love coins from <code>love share</code>, depending if you have chocolate box artifact or not. It has around 45 second cooldown.
- The id for a level 1 player can usually be found in the pin of Epic RPG Official Server #general or event chat channel  during the event
- It is not recommended to use <code>love slots</code> unless you need to complete love quest.
- If you have a lot of love coins and just time travelled, if you're in A3 or below brew a lootbox potion and spam <code>rpg love buy adventure cooldown.</code> This is the best way to obtain omega lootboxes and at the end of the tt you'll be able to have even hundreads of millions of wooden logs.

1. # **Commands**

      - /love info** - Gives an overview of the valentine's event.
      - /love share @player** - Shares a love potion with said player, healing both players and giving a small amount of love coins.
      - /love share @player notokens** - Same command as above, but it will not use the love token regardless if both player have them.
      - /love quest** - Shows the valentine's quest.
      - /love shop** - Shows the valentine's shop.
      - /love buy [item] [amount]** - Buy an item from the valentine's shop.
      - /love slots [amount]** - Gamble your love coins in a slots minigame.
      - /love top** - Shows the love coins leaderboards.

1. # **Love quest**
The love quest is the main objective of the event, and there are 5 tasks required to complete the quest:

- Find the rare mob in hunt | 0/1

- Win at least 100 love coins in love slots | 0/1

- Use 3 items to spawn a random event | 0/3

- Share a life potion with a level 1 player | 0/1

- Share a life potion 80 times | 0/80
Upon completing the quest, the player will receive a 1000 love coins, 3  Love Tokens and a Pink Fish (Ver: Pets#Pink%20Fish) pet.

1. # **Love tokens**
The love token is pretty much the best reward, alongside with the valentine's quest. It is a consumable item obtained in this event, and to use it you have to love share with another player that also has a love token. To not use token, use **love share @player notokens** to not use them.

Upon using the love token, both players will receive: 

- 15  EPIC coins.
- 80  Arena cookies.
- 6  EDGY lootboxes.
- 2 Rare lootoboxes.
- **dungeon**, **arena** and **horse** cooldown resets.

1. #  Flirt 
The valentine event added a new move to dungeon 1-9 called flirt. By flirting with the dragon, player have a 70% chance to instantly end the    dungeon regardless of stat and not use their dungeon cooldown. However, if they flirt fail then everyone instantly die and lose the dungeon

Note: As of valentine 2026, starting dungeon using slash command (/dungeon @user) will most likely freeze the dungeon and causes player to time out. It's recommended to use the **rpg** prefix in order to avoid this issue
