# Void

= VOID =

1. #  **What is Void?** 
The VOID is basically referred to as Areas 16-20. These Areas can be unlocked after defeating the EPIC NPC fight in the TOP, which gives us an EPIC JUMP, used to move to Area 16+ (if unsealed) and grants the ability to buy EPIC JUMPS from **/shop**, which is permanent. Each of these Areas are uniquely different in their own aspects, as they all have different **"Debuffs"** which can be quite difficult to deal with which makes these areas extremely difficult. 

Void Areas are also quite interesting as they have some **"requirements"**  for unsealing them which allows an area to be open for a few days. All players can contribute to these requirements by getting to the TOP and doing **/void contribute** **(area) (item) (amount).**

Another interesting thing about void is the fact that if A20 is unsealed, all players in the game have a 0.01% chance to encounter a A20 mob in lower areas.

- **Debuffs** **(Area 16 - 20)**


- **VOID AURA (*all areas to area 16 - 20) =*** It limits a player's stats and gets weaker as you advance to further areas. XP gained from bosses is strong enough to avoid this limit. This limit does not apply to equipment enchantments or horse buffs.
- It also adds a significantly high chance to get 1 rare item with working commands, the chance increases in higher areas,

(Area 16 if you're in the TOP Area) The requirements of unsealing the areas will get relatively easy than their first requirements.

1. #  **Dungeons 16 - 20** 
Once, the requirements of an area are completed, players can unseal it by **/void unseal [area]**.

The Dungeons in Void Areas are similar to those before Dungeon 10 (Dungeon 1-9), however they are accessed with void dungeon and the difficulty level is way higher and requires more players to enter.

- Completing VOID dungeons for the first time grant a tier of the Time travel bonus.
- Completing VOID dungeons provides an EPIC jump.
- Time travel bonus makes players get an extra time travel every certain amount of time travels, depending on the tier (for example, if you defeat Dungeon 16 once, Every 5 Time Travels, it will give you a free Time Travel).
- There is a chance to get the  VOIDdog which has the skill **RESETTER**  It has the chance to reset **all** pet adventures that were sent with the VOIDdog, if the pet also has a Time Traveler skill and it triggers.

- The chance to get  VOIDog increases depending on the dungeon.

1. ##  Recommendations for VOID dungeons and areas (up to now) 

- If you have enough flasks and materials, you can brew many potions, such as void potion that increases your chance to drop higher tier lootboxes and dark energy. However, you should not make jumpy potion unless you want to get a godly card because the void aura gives only 1 item and a jumpy potion increases it only by 50%. Therefore, the result is 1.50 and It's actually rounded down, so you will still get 1 item from the void aura.
- If you're in A16, you shouldn't heal because it drains 1% of your max HP. You can circuvement this by purchasing life boosts from the shop or by brewing a potion potion.
