# Epic coins

Epic coins are a currency that is used exclusively in the Epic shop. 

They represent the shape of a regular coin, but with a starry, galaxy shade instead of golden. 

Unlike regular coins, Epic coins cannot be given (Ver: Commands) to other players, nor can they be sold/traded for regular coins. 

Epic coins are obtainable through lootboxes (Ver: Lootbox), after 7 daily streaks, voting (Ver: Vote) for the bot, some special events (Ver: Special Events) and God drops (Ver: Regular Events#God Events), giving them a fairly uncommon rarity.

You can also get epic coins via patreon (Mega donator and above) and by donating in the official support server.
