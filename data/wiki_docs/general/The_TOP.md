# The TOP

Congratulations! You've managed to made the TIME dragons kill each other and in the meantime, enjoyed some cookies. Now it's time to solve the mysteries of the TOP and the VOID. 

1. #  TOP 
Defeating D15.2 (Ver: Dungeon 15.2) granted you with 1 TIME dragon essence, which you can now use to forge the GODLY cookie and enter VOID (Ver: Void) areas. You also traded all your materials into logs, which you can now fish, apples and rubies for cheaper prices! A new badge (Ver: Achievements#Badges), the TOP badge, is now available for you to claim it and use it in your profile. There are also some new commands you can use:
| Commands | Description |
| --- | --- |
| rpg epic npc | the EPIC NPC lets you know that there's a fight you can enter, and tells you about the VOID and the final fight... |
| rpg void | explains about void areas, dungeons and the content of a VOID lootbox |
| rpg void debuffs | explains the debuffs of each area and the VOID aura |
| rpg void areas | shows requirements to unseal each area, and how much longer for an area to seal again if it's unsealed |


1. #  EPIC NPC fight 
After forging and eating the GODLY cookie, you will enter the EPIC NPC fight, a complicated dungeon that you can enter and defeat in order to get an EPIC jump to enter VOID areas and unlock EPIC jumps in the shop. 

1. ##  Recommendations/requirements to enter 
- 4 TX pets to send during the fight (3 must have EPIC skill, only 1 of them can have the Time Traveler skill, and if it has Time Traveler is must also has EPIC).

- 25,000 HP.

- T9+ horse (T10 recommended).

- Around 10~15m wooden logs to sell during the fight.

- GODLY cookie (GODLY sword + 3 TIME dragon essence + 3,000 arena cookies).

- No equipment (having a sword or an armor equipped results in instant death).

- No pets on adventure before the fight

- 1,000+ life potions

- TT25+

1. ##  Fight time limit 
1 hour and 40 minutes.

1. ##  EPIC NPC fight logic 

1. ###  Phase 1 (100-90 HP) 
    - Dodge** when turn is not prime, **punch** when turn is prime.

1. ###  Phase 2 (89-65 HP) 
    - Dodge** when turn is prime or x50, when approaching from left and turn is odd or when approaching from right and turn is even.

    - Punch** when approaching from left and turn is even, or when approaching from right and turn is odd.

1. ###  Phase 3 (64-30 HP) 
    - Dodge** when turn is prime or x50.

    - Accelerate** when Mammoth  is not close to the player.

    - Punch** when Mammoth  is next to the player. 

1. ###  Phase 4 (29-10 HP) 
    - Dodge** when turn is prime or x50 (must have the same amount of T10 pets on adventure as rockets launched by the Epic NPC).

    - Return** (must have exactly 1 fewer T10 pets on adventure than rockets launched by the Epic NPC).

1. ###  Phase 5 (9-2 HP) 
You lose health over time due to injuries, use '/heal' to keep you safe.

    - Dodge** when turn is prime or x50.

    - Dodge** when turn is even.

    - Approach** when turn is odd and Mammoth  is not close to player.

    - Punch** when turn is odd and Mammoth is next to the player.

Need to '/sell' around 10~15m wooden logs before the first punch.

1. ###  Phase 6 (1-0 HP) 
You are still losing health over time due to injuries, use '/heal' to keep you safe.

    - Punch** is the only possible move in here.

The chance of succeeding is based on your coolness.


    - Notes:**

- x50 turn means multiples of 50 (50, 100, 150, 200, etc.)

- Movements logic above are ordered by priority, this means that, for example, in P5 if turn is even and Mammoth  is next to player but the turn is prime you got to **dodge** instead of **punch**

- The amount of logs you have to sell for Phase 5, theoretically, depends on the items in your inventory. 10m logs can be enough if that's the only thing in your inventory other than crops, ultra logs, super fish (will be useful if you're aiming to beat D16 afterwards), life potions and OMEGA/GODLY lootboxes. If you're also keeping other tiers of fish and logs, or apples and bananas, and common-edgy lootboxes, the amount of logs you have to sell may increase.
