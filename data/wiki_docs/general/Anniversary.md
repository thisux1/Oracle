# Anniversary

The anniversary event takes place in March 15, when the bot was released. Since then, there has been 2 anniversary events in Epic RPG. This event does not bring new commands, but it does give a lot of rewards for pretty much nothing! This event only lasts for a few days.

To celebrate the one year anniversary of the bot, players will receive an anniversary lootbox for each duel completed. In addition, every cooldown except vote has a reduction of 50% for the first day, which decreases by 10% per day until the end of the event. The same goes for monster drops - for the first day you will be able to drop 5x more monster drops. It decreases by 1 per day.

Both players in the duel will receive an anniversary lootbox, regardless if they lost or won. Each anniversary lootboxgrants one of the following items:

- 1 Hamster Pet (Ver: Pets#Hamster) - Only obtainable once. Chances to get hamster increases for each anniversary lootbox opened!
- 1  GODLY lootbox
- 1  OMEGA lootbox
- 8  EDGY lootboxes
- 15  EPIC coins
- 400  Arena cookies
- 15  Time Cookies
- 2Flasks
- 5  Party Poppers
- 30 EPIC Berries
- 1  wishing token (Ver: WIshing Token)

1. ## **Small notes**
Opening an anniversary lootbox gives 500 lootboxer XP and a chance for 1 coolness, though only if the player has unlocked coolness previously. 

Anniversary lootboxes will **never** expire, nor will dissapear upon time travel. But it will not be possible to get hamster pet from the anniversary lootboxes once the event ends.

1. ## Wishing
Wishing tokens are obtained from <code>daily</code> (5 tokens each) and anniversary lootboxes (1 token each). They can be used to wish for a reward (<code>rpg use wishing token</code>) . You have a small chance of getting any rewards, but they are very useful and rare! Each wish consumes 1 wishing token.
