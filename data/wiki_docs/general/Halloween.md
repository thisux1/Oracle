# Halloween

It's time... The spooky season has come, even into Epic RPG! Halloween event is a special event that occurs each October on the bot, and brings new items, mechanics, rewards and even a special pet! The miniboss cooldown was reduced by 4 hours (33%) hours during the time that the event lasted, and a few new mechanics, items and rewards were introduced. So far, there have been 3 halloween events in the game.

1. # Commands    
      - /hal info** - Displays information about this event on certain topics.
      - /hal buy [item]** **[amount]** - Used to buy items from the halloweeen shop.
      - /hal craft [item]** **[amount]** - Used to craft items in the new halloween recipes.
      - /hal use [item]** - Used to use the halloween items.
      - /hal slots [amount]** - Used to gamble carved pumpkins, the currency of this event.
      - /hal boo <@player>** - Used to scare other players to get pumpkins or event items.
      - /hal tasks** - Shows tasks to complete for a reward.
      - /hal quest** - Shows your current progress with the halloween quest.
      - /hal top** - Used to display a leaderboard of players who have the most pumpkins in their inventory.
      - /hal title shop/quest** - Used to equip a title.
      - /hal shop** - Displays all items of the halloween shop.
      - /hal summon** - Summons a pumpkin bat boss by using a spooky scroll.
      - /hal recipes** - Displays a list of all halloween recipes.
      - /hal wb** - Shows current progress with the world boss, and codes unlocked.
      - /hal wb [throw]** - Used to throw a monster soul to fight the world boss.

1. # Pumpkin bat boss fight
After summoning a  **pumpkin bat boss** by crafting a  spooky scroll (<code>rpg hal craft spooky scroll</code>) you start a fight with that hostile enemy. The pumpkin bat has 120 max HP and you (as a player) have 50 max HP. There are 4 options to use during the fight, and to win, you have to pick them in a specific way:

    - Attack from...**

| **The right** | **Ahead** | **The left** | **Behind** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Attack (-10 ) | Bazooka (-25 ) | Pumpkin (-1 ) | Dodge (-10 ) |

- Note: The red heart represents pumpkin bat's HP. It's reduced after each attack.*

After you achieve victory, it turns out that the pumpkin bat can't actually die, it only loses the battle. However, the pumpkin bat is  impressed for your success, and gives you an awesome reward:
- 200  pumpkins
- 100  arena cookies
- 2  EDGY lootboxes
- 3  EPIC berries
- Drops a  **Vampire teeth A** artifact if it was the first battle
    - Even in case of failure and your death, pumpkin bat is still going to give you a small reward.**

1. # World boss
 **THE EVIL PUMPQUEEN** 

This is a world boss that is always active. She has a lot of hp (5m). Try to defeat this boss by throwing  **monster souls** at her!

Each time the Boss is defeated, it will unlock a code. Up to 5 codes can be unlocked (<code>worldboss6</code> is a visual bug). To see more about the fight, go to the Official EPIC RPG Server using <code>rpg invite</code>.
1. # Tasks and quest
The possible daily tasks were:    

- Find pumpkins in training.
- Heal with a life potion.
- Check the **hal top** command.
- Eat a cookie.
- Fish 4 times.
- Chop 4 times.
- Fail to scare a player.
- Succesfully scare 2 players with **hal boo**.
- Get scared by another player with **hal boo**.
- Use this command twice.
- Vote for the bot.
- Open a lootbox.
- Trade with the EPIC NPC.
- Use the **rules** command.
- Use the **invite** command.
- Find help of 3 commands.

Upon completion, they would award 100 pumpkins + 2 EPIC candies. Aditionally, a minimum of 3-7 damage was added on a hunt to be able to complete the heal with a life potion task.

The possible weekly tasks were:    

- Find a bat slime.
- Buy 5 lootboxes.
- Use the **weekly** command.
- Start 3 arenas.
- Start or boost 5 **miniboss** fights.
- Defeat 3 Sleepy Jack O' Lantern.
- Fail to defeat a Sleepy Jack O' Lantern.
- Summon the PUMPKIN BAT BOSS.
- Buy an item in **hal shop**.
- Find a monster soul.
- Craft a candy bell.

Upon completion, the tasks would award 500 pumpkins + 5 EPIC candies. 

The quest required from the player to complete 4 fundamental tasks which were:  

- Summon 10 pumpkin bat bosses.
- Claim 25 tasks rewards.
- Craft 12 candy bells.
- Throw 5 souls to the world boss.

Upon successful completion, it would grant these rewards to the player:      

- Pumpkin Bat pet (Ver: Pets#Pumpkin%20Bat)
- 15 Sleepy potions
- 5000 pumpkins
- 50 spooky orbs
- "<code>This is the best title</code>"
