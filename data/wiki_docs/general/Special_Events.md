# Special Events

Special events are large, themed global events that are time-limited and celebrate some sort of event or holiday. Special events can be checked by using the <code>rpg event</code> command.
1. # **Seasonal Events**
Seasonal events are time-limited events that last for a certain period of time and will not appear again until the next year. There are 6 seasonal events that will occur every time each year. The 6 being:

- Christmas
- Easter
- Halloween
- Horse Festival
- Valentine
- Anniversary

1. # **Returning event**
A returning event is when the player has spent some time without doing a single command from the bot, this event will bring the "returning player" some decent loot for the player to feel comfortable to play again~ During the returning quest, the player will gain a 33% cooldown reduction, have double the chance for monsters to drop items, not requiring any keys for dungeons and claiming amazing rewards daily! The event will last a week.
1. ## **Commands**

      - /returning info** - Gives an overview about the returning event
      - /returning superdaily** - Gives a daily reward for a week
      - /returning quest** - Shows the returning quest
      - /returning shop** - Displays the returning shop
      - /returning buy** - Buys an item from the returning shop

1. ##  **Super Daily**
The player will receive a reward each day after the return event is triggered, these rewards are:

- 1  OMEGA lootbox - 1st day
- 5  EDGY lootboxes - 2nd day
- 1  Magic bed - 3rd day
- 3 OMEGA Horse Token - 4th day
- 250  Arena cookies - 5th day
- 5  EDGY lootboxes - 6h day
- 5 OMEGA Horse Token - 7th day

1. ## **Returning quest**
Completing the quest will give some rewards to the player, to complete the quest, the player must complete 3 simple tasks, these tasks are:

- Collect 40  smol coins
- Buy 25 items in the returning shop
- Claim 3 returning super daily rewards

The rewards from the quest are:

- 1  OMEGA lootbox
- 10 Arena tokens
- 250  Arena cookies
- 25  EPIC coins
- 200 smol coins

Smol coins are obtainable through hunt, adventure and working commands.

1. # **Compensation Events**
This is a list of the compensation events that have occurred due major bugs or issues.

1. ## **Compensation event #1**
This event started on June 27, 5:42 UTC, and ended on July 1, 5:42 UTC. This event lasted for 4 days.

Due to temporary issues with the bot, the cooldown of the hunt was increased to 2 min. As compensation for this issue, this event was held. Players were able to receive Compensation Lootbox every time they dueled with a 100% drop chance for both players. In addition, the following bonuses were in effect:

- <code>Hunt</code> gives x2 XP and coins
- Chance to tier up using <code>pets fusion</code> increased by x1.25

Both players in the duel will receive a lootbox. Each compensation lootbox granted one of the following items:

- 6 monster drops (Depends on what area you currently are in, not your max area)
- 5 Epic Coins
- 1 Edgy Lootbox
- 100 Epic Coins
- OMEGA lootbox
- A Tier 1 Hamster Pet

Additionally, opening a Compensation Lootbox also provides 200 Lootboxer XP.

    - Note**: **C<i>ompensation lootboxes were given no matter what lootbox you had previously.</i>**

1. # **500k servers**
As the bot reached closer to being in 500k servers, the Developer launched an event that started in January 31th, 2022 and ended at February 5th, 2022. The event introduced an exclusive currency for the event and multiple rewards for the event. ALL (except for daily and weekly) cooldowns had a 25% reduction while the event lasted.

The main objective of the event is to get  celebration coins as it is the main currency of the event. To obtain these, there is multiple methods such as hunting, fishing or going into an adventure. 

The new mob that takes part in this event was the  party slime which is found in hunts and rewards 10,000 celebration coins upon encounter. Another method of finding celebration coins is the celebration daily quest, which is doing basic tasks in exchange for 25,000 celebration coins, these tasks can vary from giving coins to a player or voting for the bot and there are 5 different possible daily tasks, which are randomized. Want to multiply your celebration coins? Then why don't do it? The "multiply" command is able to multiply your celebration coins by 105% and can be used once every 12 hours.

- Note: The maximum amount of celebrations coins you can hold is 500k.

1. #  New Year Event 
This is an event that started on 1st of January 2023 and lasted for a Week, during this time, a new item was introduced being the  Party Popper alongside a 25% cooldown reduction!

Upon using a  Party Popper the player is provided a few boosts for 1 hour

- 15% extra chance to drop lootboxes
- 30% chance to get better tier of materials in Working Commands
- 20% time reduction on pet adventure time

Party Poppers are obtained through the Daily command and very rarely from lootboxes.
