# Easter

Easter is a seasonal event that takes place around April each year, bringing up time-limited commands, items, mechanics and rewards! Find eggs by hunting, fight event bosses, and catch bunnies along the way! Maybe you'll even find a special golden bunny. 
1. # **Commands**
      - /egg eat** - Used to eat easter eggs as a replacement for life potions.
      - /egg info** - Gives information on topics like bunnies and items.
      - /egg shop** - Displays the egg shop.
      - /egg buy [item] [amount]** - Used to buy items in the egg shop.
      - /egg use [item]** - Uses an easter item.
      - /egg slots [amount]** - Gamble your easter eggs.
      - /egg tasks** - Shows daily and weekly tasks to complete for rewards.
      - /egg top** - Shows a global leaderboard of easter eggs.
      - /egg recipes** - Shows a list of recipes of easter items.
      - /egg craft [item] [amount]** - Craft easter items or an artifact.
      - /egg quest** - Shows your current progress with Easter quest.
      - /egg title shop/quest** - Equips the shop/quest title.
      - /egg wb** - Shows current progress with the world boss, and codes unlocked.
      - /egg wb [throw]** - Uses a pink egg to fight the world boss.
      - /egg god** - Summons a Bunny God. Requires 7 round eggs in inventory.
      - /egg open** - Used to open an Easter lootbox.

1. # World Boss
 **THE BIG EGG** 

This is a global boss that is active at all times. With its massive amount of HP (25m), players will have to try and defeat the boss by throwing  pink eggs at it!

Each time the Boss is killed, it will unlock a code. Up to 5 codes can be unlocked. To see more about the fight, go to the Official EPIC RPG Server using <code>rpg invite</code>.

1. ### Catching a bunny
A bunny  event is very similar to a training pet event . However, bunny is not a real pet because it jumps into your inventory if caught, therefore It's not increased by a higher tier horse or time travel.    

- The bunny has a happiness and hunger stat.
- 1 <code>feed</code> decreases  hunger by 18-22
- 1 <code>pat</code> increases  happiness by 8-12.
- If happiness is 85+ higher than hunger, catch chance is 100%.
- You can only use up to 6 actions.
- You will get 15  easter eggs for every command not used.
If you are not experienced and are not sure if you'll be able to catch it, you can use a helper bot.

1. #  Notes, tips & trivia        

1. If you want to get round eggs and bunnies easily, craft rainbow cookies (You can have 30 max in your inventory) and use hunt, working commands and adventure frequently.
1. The fastest way to get easter eggs is camping on a bunny boss spawning channel and frequently fighting. This way you may even get thousands of eggs in a hour! If you want to get more eggs from commands, get egg gear.
1. The egg world boss is a female.
1. The powerful egg blessing received from bunny god lasts 30 days (a full month) and provides:
- +20 LIFE,
- +20 AT,
- +20 DEF,
- active auto healing,
- +60% LV XP,
- +60% coins,
- +35% lootbox drops,
- +35% monster drops,
- +35% event drops,
- +10% profession XP,
- +30% working items,
- +10% enchant luck.
5. There is a bug that allows you to interact with someone else's bunny and try to catch it, however, the bunny will jump to their inventory if you'll attempt to do so.
