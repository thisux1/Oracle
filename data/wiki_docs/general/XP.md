# XP

Players can obtain XP (Experience) in commands to level up and increase their ATK, DEF and LIFE.

1. # How to get free XP
XP is obtained from hunt, adventure, quest, epic quest (Ver: Quest#Epic Quest), duel, miniboss (Ver: Regular Events#Miniboss), training and eating arena cookies (Ver: Regular Events#Arena). It is also possible to level up through certain events (Ver: Regular Events#Random_Events) and cook recipes (Ver: Crafting#Cooking).

Upon dying, a level will be lost and the player will start with 0% XP progress. Please note that tier IV and higher horses (Ver: Horse) automatically always grant immortality, preventing death in hunts and adventures, but it will not grant any rewards for a failed hunt.
