# Christmas

The christmas event starts on December 1st and ends on January 5th, introduces a lot of items, a new area with their own mobs, a dungeon and even a WORLD BOSS!

1. # Commands

      - rpg xmas info** - Gives a basic overview of the xmas event.
      - rpg xmas tree** - Shows current progress of xmas tree.
      - rpg xmas calendar** - Check this command daily for daily rewards!
      - rpg xmas quest** - Check your current progress with xmas quest.
      - rpg xmas tasks** - Daily and weekly tasks to complete for rewards.
      - rpg xmas shop** - See the xmas shop.
      - rpg xmas buy [item] [amount]** - Buy an item from the xmas shop.
      - rpg xmas use [item]** - Use a xmas item.
      - rpg xmas chimney** - It gives a random present tier or snowballs, can be used every 3 hours, can also make you get stuck in there for 5 minutes or just do nothing
      - rpg xmas presents** - Shows a list of presents and basic information about them.
      - rpg xmas open [present] [amount]** - Open your presents with this command. You can open up to 10 presents at a time.
      - rpg xmas dungeon [@players]** - To enter the dungeon of the christmas area.
      - rpg xmas top** - Shows the global leaderboard of snowflakes.
      - rpg xmas craft [item] [amount]** - Craft a xmas item.
      - rpg xmas recipes** - Shows a list about xmas recipes.
      - rpg xmas slots [amount]** - Gamble your presents.
      - rpg xmas recycle [item] [amount]** - Turn your items into snowballs.
      - rpg xmas title [title]** - To activate obtained titles during the event.
      - rpg xmas wb [throw]** - To fight the world boss of the event.

1. # Christmas tree 
Players can complete their christmas trees to receive the best reward of the event, including gingerbreads, GODLY presents and the snowball pet. 

To complete the Christmas tree, the following list of items is required:

- **Tree base**: 1
      - Pine needles**: 90
      - Ornaments**: 40
- **Christmas star**: 1

Upon completion, it would give the rewards on December 25th at 00:00 UTC.

- 10 VOID presents
- 25 GODLY presents
- 6,000  Arena Cookies
- 50  Time Cookies
- 4  Christmas Socks
- 10  Gingerbreads
- 40,000 Snowballs
- 2,500 Snowflakes
- 1 Snowball Pet (Ver: Pets#Snowball)
- Christmas title tree "this is the best title"   

    - NOTE**: If the christmas tree wasn't completed by December 25th, the reward given depended on the percentage of completeness.

1. # The Hellishrishmas Star
The  **Hellishrishmas Star** is an event with complicated mechanics. It spawns randomly in all servers on a specific cluster. There are 32 clusters in total. You can check the cluster you are on with <code>rpg info</code>. During an <code>adventure</code>, you may find random clues about the location of the star. The clues are:

      - This place is full of footprints, he's here**
      - You find many footprints, they are all on this eighth...**
      - You find some footprints, they are all on this quarter...**
      - You find a few footprints, they are all on this half side...**

1. # Christmas quest
This event brought a Quest, which would give rewards, but essentially, the five star parts required to make the star for the tree. The quest required from the player to complete 5 simple steps in order to complete it, those being:

1. ##  There is no such thing as "too many"

      - Requirement**: Open 600 presents.
      - Reward**:  First part of christmas star,  20 MEGA presents.

1. ##  A special dragon 

      - Requirement**: Complete the christmas dungeon twice.
      - Reward**:  Second part of christmas star,  5 ULTRA present.

1. ##  Not the most common slime 

      - Requirement**: Find the rare mob in hunt () 3 times.
      - Reward**:  Third part of christmas star,  OMEGA present.

1. ##  Daily 2.0 

      - Requirement**: Claim 5 rewards of the advent calendar.
      - Reward**:  Fourth part of christmas star,  4 Snowbox.

1. ##   Christmas tree

      - Requirement**: Complete 75% of the christmas tree.
- **Reward**:  Fifth part of the christmas star,  1000 snowflakes.

1. #  Christmas area (Area 0)
Area 0 is a special area available only during the christmas event, only accessible by using a  Gingerbread. This area has christmas related mobs, which give no XP nor coins. However, they have 100% chance to give 2 christmas related items from each hunt/adventure, monster drops are dependant on max area (Example: if the maximum area unlocked is 3, mobs in area 0 will drop zombie eyes), and chopping working commands bring x2 worker XP. However, fish related commands do not give any items in this area because the lakes are frozen. This is not all, though, as training gives 3x XP and there is a **permanent** cooldown reduction of 10%!

1. #  Christmas dungeon 
This is a special dungeon available only in the christmas area. There are two additional requirements to enter this dungeon: players should form a team of 3 and each player must craft the  Christmas key. The difficulty of this dungeon depends on the highest level player in the team. All players must survive in order to successfully pass the dungeon. Upon completing the dungeon, the players will receive 1 random Mythic xmas item as reward.

    - NOTE**: If the dungeon fails, the players can re-use the Christmas key to attempt again.

1. ## **  Santa Gear**
There's a catch, though. Santa Gear will increase your stats in the Christmas dungeon but it will also give a few benefits if all players in the dungeon have the gear

- Succeding in the dungeon will increase the Max Area of all players up to A10
- Dungeon Cooldown **will** be required
- The dungeon boss will be weaker
- There are more rewards of significant amount of presents and snowballs, including a chance to drop another Christmas key  (65%)

1. # The PRESENTIENT


The christmas world boss spawns on December 8th and lasts for the rest of the christmas event or until her 5th phase is defeated.

All players can participate in fighting the world boss by throwing  Holly leaves using <code>xmas wb throw</code> command. The World boss will appear 5 times during the event and each time the boss is defeated, a new code will be revealed up to 5 times. Status of the world boss fight can be tracked using <code>xmas wb command</code>.
