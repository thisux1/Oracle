# Coins

Coins can be used for things such as shop, giving (to other players) and Gambling. The different types of coins are coins, epic coins and guild rings. 

1. #  **Coins** 

1. ###  **Shop** 
Coins can be spent in the shop.

1. ###  **Giving** 
Coins can be given to other players with the **/give [player name] [amount]** command. There are 2 things however that may interfere with this, the giving cap (Ver: Coins Cap) and the time travel restrictions. 

The time travel differences are for TT0-1, TT2-19 and TT20+. A player of one of these, may give coins to another player as long as they are in the same category (e.g. TT0-1). However, if they try to give coins to a player fitting a different category, the transaction won't go through. This is to prevent high TT players giving coins to lower TT players and boost them in their journey, as they should be working for this themselves.

1. ###  **Gambling** 
The use of coins in gambling can be found under gambling.

1. ###  **Others** 
Coins can be deposited into your bank account and give an XP boost in hunts and adventures, the XP boost given is based on the amount of coins in your bank account.

Coins can be used in duel if the CC (Credit Card) option is picked. The recommended amount of coins to have when picking this is around 10b as it would overpower other options picked.

Coins can be used to upgrade your horse. The amount of coins depends on your lootboxer profession level and the level of the horse.

Coins can be used for recipes such as crafting (Ver: Crafting#Armors) the Coin armor or forging (Ver: Void#Void Armors & Swords) the Space armor.

1. #  **Epic coins** 
Epic coins can be spent in the epic shop. The epic shop can be found under shop.

1. #  **Guild coins** 
Guild rings can be spent in the guild shop. The guild shop can be found under Guilds.
