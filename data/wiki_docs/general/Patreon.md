# Patreon

1. # **Description**
Patreon is a third-party website that allows players to pay real money to the developer in exchange for benefits, and it can be accessed at **/patreon**. The benefits change based on the patreon tier the player chooses; they might vary from shorter cooldowns to access to a few exclusive commands and even a donor role on the Official EPIC RPG Server!  

If the player gets charged twice because they forgot to cancel the subscription, it is possible to ask for a refund through the patreon website.  

It's important to note that Patreon always charges on the first of each month, regardless of when the subscription is bought, so players should keep that in mind. 

1. # **Benefits**
As stated previously, by purchasing any patreon donor tier and supporting the developer, the player will receive their respective benefits. These rewards change based on which tier is purchased, and include the following benefits:
| Benefits | Donator | EPIC Donator | SUPER Donator | MEGA Donator | HYPER Donator | ULTRA Donator | ULTIMATE Donator |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cooldown Reduction | 10% | 20% | 35% | 35% | 35% | 35% | 35% |
| Rewards with Daily and Weekly (Coins) | x1.2 | x1.4 | x1.7 | x1.7 | x1.7 | x1.7 | x1.7 |
| Rewards with Duel | x2 | x2 | x2 | x2 | x2 | x2 | x2 |
| Extra Commands | No | Coins, Life, Stats, Progress, Equipment | All | All | All | All | All |
| Monthly Epic Coins | No | No | No | 120 | 500 | 1250 | 2800 |


Except for Epic Coins and Rewards, almost all of the benefits remain the same after Super Donor. The Marriage (Ver: Marry) command, which is available after Super Donator or better, allows the player to find a partner to hunt for them and vice versa.
