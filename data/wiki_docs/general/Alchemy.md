# Alchemy

1. # **What is Alchemy?**
Alchemy is a very important system on Epic RPG, it gives boosts that are very useful for players and it is generally advised to have a good look into it. The command is unlocked upon reaching Area 7 and it can be used with the command **alchemy (potion name).** 

It is possible to get flasks through 7 day streak of vote and daily, weekly commands, card hand and special deals from epic shop. Alongside this, flasks **are lost** upon time travelling so it's generally advised to make use of them before a Time Travel as the boosts are **kept** and you'll have a chance of dropping a card.

1. # **Recipes**
Each potion uses 1 flask, except Triple potion which requires 3 flasks.

1. # Descriptions of potions and recommendations
Most of the players usually make potions at the end of TT because this is the moment when you have a lot of flasks and can prepare potions for the next TT with a small chance of getting cards. Sometimes, they are also useful in low areas, for example when you need to prepare for a dungeon. Here are descriptions of all potions and recommendations in order:

    - Beginner potions**  

      - Banana potion** - It's helpful if you want to level up fast and get more resurces, especially if you're below TT2.
      - Triple potion** - It is quite expensive considering the fact that it requires 3 flasks to brew, but It's useful if you are farming crops for STT score or cooking recipes during an ultraining run.
      - Inverted potion** - If you need to complete guild tasks or get better enchantments, this is a good potion because it lasts for a long time and doesn't require a lot of materials.
      - Potion potion** - If you need more HP for a dungeon or want to auto-heal every hunt, this is a good potion.
      - Monster potion** - It can be used in many ways and It's decent for all TT's because it gives a 30% monster drop chance (including epic berries) and 10% lootbox drop chance (if your lootbox drop chance is above 100%, it increases the chance of getting a better lootbox instead). It also gives a small XP bonus and more coins from <code>hunt</code> and <code>adventure</code>.
      - Smol potion** - It's recommended to get this after time travelling because with this potion you'll get a lot more levels and therefore you'll have more HP for D11-D14. Can be also used to level up faster as a low TT player.
    - Simple potions**   

      - Liquid hair potion** - It's hard to tell what the purpose of this potion is because It's very expensive (60 mermaid hairs - don't brew it if you do not have many time travels) and it only lasts for 5h with minor bonuses. If you have spare flasks and lots of mermaid hairs, you can use it if you want to get a little bit better enchantments, however an inverted potion is way better for this.
      - Juice potion** - It's useful if you need more stats for your current area or the next dungeon. It also lasts for a very long time compared to other potions.
- **Wood potion** - if you're above TT5 and need more profession xp, it is a very good potion to make before cooking recipes and time travelling because it gives a boost to profession XP, therefore you'll get more xp from banana pickaxes. After time travelling you can also use this bonus to level up your enchanting profession (transcending is very cheap in A1) and to get better items with working commands (if you're ascended - use dynamite)
      - Fish potion** - Same recommendations as for wood potion.
      - p2w potion** - Its name is literal because this potion is used to convert epic coins into coins. You can sell apples at the end of your TT and you'll get a lot of money for that. However, if you do not want to make this potion to sell items for this purpose, don't brew it because It's expensive.
      - Flask potion** - Considering the fact that it only gives 0.01% cooldown reduction, which is literally like nothing, lume probably added this as a joke. Don't brew it unless you have spare flasks and want a card.
    - Advanced potions**   

      - Dragon breath potion** - If you're TT25+ and need dragon scales for D11-D15 gear or coolness for D15-2 and epic npc fight, brew it.
      - Cookie potion** - If you're TT2+, like pets and would like to get more pets for D15 and epic npc fight, brew it. Otherwise save your cookies for super cookies and christmas event.
      - Lootbox potion** - Honestly it is most likely one of the best potions in this game, especially during events like Valentine. During this event all you have to do is brewing it at the end of your TT, time travelling, and spamming <code>rpg love buy adventure cooldown</code>. After you'll finish doing it, you'll most likely have dozens of omega lootboxes in your inventory. Open them in A3 and follow trade rates, this way you'll have a lot of materials at the end of the next TT. But even outside special events this potion is still good, it gives a huge bonus to lootbox drops. Keep hunting and adventuring and for sure you'll get a lot of lootboxes. However, It's recommended to brew it only if you're ascended due to the fact that it requires an omega lootbox.
      - Electronical potion** - Despite the fact that the amount of chips you need to make this potion seems to be absurd, if you and your partner are very active you may get 2222 chips in 3 days! You can benefit from this potion a lot during an ultraining run, as it gives many boosts that last for a day. Therefore, you should only brew it if you're an endgamer (TT25+).
    - ENDGAME potions**  

      - King potion** - This potion has the same purpose as a p2w potion. It's more expensive, but more effective.
      - Void potion** - If you're in void, It's quite useful due to the fact that its boosts guarantee more items refunded from contribution (epic berries and arena cookies) and a higher chance to drop high tier lootboxes (omega and godly lootboxes) that are required to unseal the next areas. The enchanting bonus doesn't matter a lot because you already have a lot of enchantment multipliers as a TT25+ endgamer.
      - Jumpy potion** - Despite its description, this potion increases the void aura drop chance by 50%.
      - Time potion** - This potion is used to trade time cookies for more materials in your inventory before a time travel. To do it correctly, time travel without using any logs for banana pickaxes and other recipes, then do correct trade rates in the next time travel. This way you'll have a lot more logs. Use it only if you have over 100 million wooden logs in your inventory and enough time cookies. Considering the fact that it lasts for 42 years (until you time travel), you can freely brew it even at the beginning of your time travel.
    - So, these are all recommendations for all potions from experienced players! Feel free to add your own if you have one!**
