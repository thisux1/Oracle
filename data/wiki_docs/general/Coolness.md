# Coolness

Coolness is a system for the player to obtain badges (see **/badge** for more), Achievements and is needed for Dungeon 15.2 and the Epic NPC fight. The Coolness system and ultraining (Ver: Training#Ultraining) are unlocked upon reaching TT1 A12. 

To view your Coolness score, simply use the "/profile" or "/ultraining progress" commands. To view the Coolness Shop, use the "/ultraining shop" command. 

1. #  Obtaining Coolness 
The main way to obtain Coolness is with **/ultraining**, which guarantees 2 coolness for every stage beaten successfully. There are other ways to obtain coolness; however, they are not guaranteed to give coolness like ultraining. The other ways to obtain coolness include:

- Going from MAX HP to 1 HP in an Adventure has a chance to grant +1 coolness.
- Getting HYPER or ULTRA logs from working commands (Ver: Working Commands) has a chance to grant +1 coolness.
- Opening an OMEGA or GODLY Lootbox has a chance to grant +1 coolness.
- Ascending your Pets has a chance to give +1 coolness.
- Forging equipment such as Ultra-Edgy gear has a chance to give +1 coolness.
- Selling dragon scales (the exact formula is yet unknown, but It's assumed that 10-14 dragon scales give 1 coolness).
- There are other yet unknown ways to obtain coolness.


The chance to get coolness from the methods above decreases as players obtain more coolness. When reaching 100 coolness, the chance will drop significantly. Players can use "/ultraining progress" to view the current amount of coolness, current progress in ultraining, current pet slots multiplier and coolrency. Coolness and ultraining stages are not reset after a time travel (Ver: Time Travel).

1. # **Coolness Shop**
On the first day of each month, players are given **coolrency** to buy things from the ultraining shop, the amount given is **equal** to the total amount of coolness the player has. To view the Coolness Shop, use the "/ultraining shop" command. It will show you the amount of coolrency available to spend. To purchase an item, use "/ultraining buy" and select the item.
