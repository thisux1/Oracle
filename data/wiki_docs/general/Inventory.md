# Inventory

The inventory displays all the items (Ver: Inventory#Items) and consumables (Ver: Inventory#Consumables) obtained.

1. # Items
Items are used in craft recipes (Ver: Crafting) and can be sold (Ver: Shop#Sell) for coins.

    - Note:**

- With enough different types of items in your inventory, a new section in the embed will appear, so other items can also fit in the embed. This section is called 'More items'.
- If you have a  Top hat, your inventory cap is multiplied by x5. For more information, see artifacts.

1. # Consumables 
Consumables are used to get items, heal, start a random event (Ver: Events#Random Events) or similar.

    - Note**: seasonal event items will be placed into a new 3rd column in the inventory, labeled after the active event.
