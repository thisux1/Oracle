# Profile

Displays a player's title, marriage partner (Ver: Marry), badges (Ver: Achievements#Badges), rank (Ver: Top) (global level), progress, stats, equipment and money

A profile background (Ver: Shop#Profile%20Backgrounds) can be used for aesthetic purposes

1. # Profile content

1. ## Progress

- Level
- XP
- Area (current & max)
- Time travels (Ver: Time Travel) (requires at least 1 time travel)

1. ## Stats

- Attack
- Defense
- Health (HP)

1. ## Equipment

- Sword (Ver: Crafting#Swords)
- Armor (Ver: Crafting#Armors)
- Horse

1. ## Money

- Coins
- EPIC Coins
- Bank (Ver: Shop#Epic%20Shop) (requires a bank account)

1. ## Others

- Title (Ver: Achievements)
- Badges (Ver: Achievements#Badges)
- Rank
- Marriage Partner (Ver: Marry)
