# Life

Life (sometimes called hitpoints, health points or HP) is a profile statistic which determines the player's ability to withstand amounts of damage dealt by various sources. The starting amount of maximum life is 100 (at level 1) and it increases by 5 for every level the player reaches. The formula for maximum life is **(100 + (level -1)*5 + cook buff + event buff + potion buff) * (100% + horse buff%) + life boost**. Life can be replenished by drinking a life potion/healing by eating bread, cooking a Baked Fish/Fruit Salad and buying a Life Boost/Event (Ver: Special Events) Boost (the action will replenish the life and once it has done so, it will add its boost on top of your current maximum life).

1. #  How to increase maximum life? 
Other than the previously mentioned life increase from levels, maximum life can also be increased by:

- Cooking Baked Fish (+5 maximum life) and Fruit Salads (+1 maximum life)
- Buying event boosts
- Buying a Life Boost from the **shop**. Life Boost A increases maximum life by 10 and costs 10 000 coins; Life Boost B increases it by 25 and costs 100 000 coins, and Life Boost C increases it by 50 and costs 1 000 000 coins.
- Getting a tank horse, whose boost increases your maximum life by a certain percentage (visible with **/horse**)
- Brewing a potion

1. #  How can life be lost? 
Life is lost by taking damage from the commands hunt and adventure, dying (losing a level), getting the bad outcome of the "Try to fix it" option in the Enchant Event, getting hurt in a Dungeon and by losing your tank horse (if you had one) in **horse breeding**.

1. #  Life(command) 
Life is a command that displays player's life, the command is only available for players that have bought Epic Donors benefits in the Epic RPG patreon website.
