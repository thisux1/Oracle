# Fighting the Epic NPC

You have reached the TOP and are looking to fight the EPIC NPC after obtaining 3 Time dragon essences. This is known as the EPIC NPC fight (TOP fight or fight, NOT a dungeon). So what will you need to do to succeed?

    - Note:** This page is only a guide.

1. #  Preparations 
Before attempting the fight, you will need...

- **4** TX (or higher) pets. At least 3 of these pets should have the **EPIC** skill, and only one of these three pets can have the **time traveler** skill. If a pet has the time traveler skill, it must also be EPIC. The pet types do not matter. No pets should be on adventure before the fight.
- **25,000+ HP** (recommended)
- **1000** life potions (recommended)
- **15m+** wooden logs in inventory*. You will sell these during the fight
- **3000+** coolness (the more you have, the higher probability of winning the fight)
- **1** GODLY cookie (you will eat this to start the fight)
- **T9 or T10**  horse (T9 horse required, but T10 recommended)

1. #  Fighting the EPIC NPC 
The time limit for the fight is 100 minutes (1h40m), so it gives you plenty of time to fight the NPC. To start the fight, type `rpg eat godly cookie` and respond with 'ok'. Make note that if you misinput once, you will be instantly killed on all Phases from 1-4*.


1. ##  Phase 1 (100-91 HP) 
In Phase 1, the EPIC NPC will begin its first volley. This phase is actually very easy when you keep in mind the **TURN NUMBER** (Turn #), making it very scripted.  The first command will always be dodge, as the number "One" is not a prime number. 
- If the Turn # is **prime**: **PUNCH**
- If the Turn # is **non-prime**: **DODGE**
- A number is **prime** if it has EXACTLY two factors

(For example, 19 is a prime number as you can only multiply 1 and 19 to get 19. So, on Turn #19, you will punch the EPIC NPC.)

This phase should end on Turn #30, where the EPIC NPC will reach 90 HP. Continue to PUNCH and DODGE until this, and you will reach Phase 2.

1. ##  Phase 2 (90-66 HP) 
Once in Phase 2, the EPIC NPC will mount his horse and start attacking you again. The fight becomes a bit more complicated here. You will have the same DODGE and PUNCH actions, but now you also have to consider the **direction** the NPC attacks you from.

You will **DODGE** when

- Turn # is **prime** or a **multiple of 50**
- Turn # is **ODD,** and the NPC is approaching from the **LEFT**
- Turn # is **EVEN**, and the NPC is approaching from the **RIGHT**

And you will only **PUNCH** when

- Turn # is **ODD**, and the NPC is approaching from the **RIGHT**
- Turn # is **EVEN**, and the NPC is approaching from the **LEFT**

(For example, here the Turn # is 30, and the NPC is approaching from the RIGHT. Because it is EVEN and RIGHT, you will DODGE this turn.)

This phrase *should* end where the NPC will reach 65 HP. Continue to punch and dodge accordingly, and you will reach Phase 3.

1. ##  Phase 3 (65-31 HP) 

Once in Phase 3, you'll suddenly decide to get on your horse to try and match the EPIC NPC's speed and hit him easier. The Phase is still lengthy, though a bit easier to navigate through than Phase 2. Along with being able to PUNCH and DODGE, you will also get to ACCELERATE to get closer to the NPC. Yes, the EPIC NPC is riding a mammoth, check its profile (rpg p epic npc).

Here, you will be able to see exactly how far the EPIC NPC's mount is from you, and that will primarily determine what move you will choose.

- If Turn # is **prime** or a **multiple of 50**, reply with DODGE
- If 🦣is **not** right next to the player, reply with ACCELERATE
- If 🦣is right next to the player, reply with PUNCH

(For example, here the Turn # is 114, and the NPC is far away from the player. You would reply with ACCELERATE, since it is non-prime you are not next to the NPC.)

As you hit the EPIC NPC for 3 "e-LIFE" (HP) on every PUNCH, except for the last punch (2 e-LIFE), this Phase *should* end where the NPC will reach 30 HP. Continue to move accordingly, and you will reach Phase 4.

1. ##  Phase 4, the Pet Phase (30-11 HP) 

In Phase 4, the EPIC NPC gains distance pulls out a rocket launcher and begins shooting rockets at you. Here is where having those Tier 10 (or higher) pets will be necessary, as you will perform some pet adventures and returns with them here. Along with PUNCH and DODGE, you will be able to RETURN rockets, given the requirements are fulfilled.

In this phase, it is important to know that you will send your pets in an adventure **first**, and **next** do the commands. Doing it the opposite way will kill you. Take note of how many rockets are being shot at you in each turn, as it will affect how many pets you send out. You will always have one pet sent out. (If you have a **time traveler** pet, this is why it is important to also have the **EPIC** skill on it.)

- If the turn is **prime** or a **multiple of 50**: DODGE
    - If you dodge, you must have the **same amount of pets** currently set out as amount of rockets the EPIC NPC sends out to you.
- Do not PUNCH the EPIC NPC. In a game of rock paper scissors, a rocket will always win.
- In all other turns of this Phase, you will reply with RETURN
    - If you RETURN, you must have **one less pet** currently on an adventure than the amount of rockets the EPIC NPC sends out to you. This is important to deal damage and not die.  For example, on Turn #167, which is a prime number, the EPIC NPC may launch 4 rockets - you will DODGE. Before you DODGE, however, you will be sure to have all 4 of your pets on an adventure. On the next turn, Turn #168, being non-prime, if the NPC launches 2 rockets, you will take 3 pets out of their adventure - leaving only ONE pet - and you will RETURN one rocket. (Note that two rockets were sent on a RETURN Turn, so you will only have one pet in an adventures.)

This is how the Pet Phase will go for the entire Phase. Each rocket returned to the NPC will deal 1 point of e-LIFE to it. Continue to DODGE, RETURN, and not PUNCH until the NPC reaches 10 HP, and you will reach Phase 5.

1. ##  Phase 5 (10-2 HP) 
    - Sell all of your wooden logs (10 to 15 million minimum) at the start of this phase with "rpg sell wooden log all"**

Y**ou will constantly take damage in this phase in real time (even if you are not running any commands), so run "rpg drink" every 20 seconds.**

    - There are items that scroll by on the top and bottom lanes that can be grabbed to pick up.  The chance to grab scales with the players time travel, so higher time travel players can more easily succeed at grabbing.  Do not attempt to grab if this is your first time beating the epic NPC, as failing a grab means you will die.  Items that can be obtained from grab include gears, which can be used for the post-VOID epic NPC fight, and time dragon essence.**

After selling logs:

1. If the turn is even or prime: DODGE
1. If you are not adjacent to the epic npc: APPROACH
1. If you are adjacent to the epic npc: PUNCH
1. # You will only be punching on ODD, NOT-PRIME turns when you are adjacent to the EPIC NPC
1. **DO NOT ATTEMPT TO GRAB UNLESS YOU HAVE ALREADY BEATEN THE FIGHT AND ARE TRYING TO FARM GEARS**

1. ##  Phase 6, the Last Phase (1 HP) 
Congratulations on making it through nearly the entire fight! Clearly, the NPC is very tired. It is not over yet though, and there is still a chance to lose as in this phase, there are only two options - PUNCH and DODGE. Remember to heal like Phase 5, since you are still losing HP over time.

- If you DODGE, you will die. But hey, at least you get an achievement for it.
- If you PUNCH, your probabilities of hitting the NPC, ending the fight and not dying are dependent on how much coolness you have.

- With 1000 coolness, you will never win the fight
- With 1500 coolness, chance to win is approximately 55.56% 
- With 3000 coolness, chance to win is approximately88.89%
- With 4500 coolness, chance to win is approximately 95.06%
- With 10000 coolness, chance to win is approximately 99%

For this reason, it is recommended to have at least 3000 coolness for the fight, as lower coolness seems to associate with less chance to win. It is also for this reason that the more you have, the more likely you will win so going over will never hurt.

1. #  Rewards 
If you succeed, the NPC will end the fight and declare you the winner, and give you an  EPIC jump, which is required to get to the VOID, and progress through its individual areas. Additionally, you will be able to purchase an EPIC jump in the `rpg shop` from now on, for 2 TIME dragon essence.
