1. # Arena remake - 06/12/2019
Now in arena you get cookies instead of xp and you can eat them to get xp and added a ranking for amount of cookies. balanced captcha rewards, fixed other bugs and typos

1. # Minor fix - 06/12/2019
fixed a bug with epic coins.

1. # Minor fix - 06/11/2019
Fixed a bug with marriage in profile, divorce and take. 

1. # Minor balances - 06/11/2019
The jail and captcha rewards were reduced as well as the chance to get the captcha in hunt and adventure. 

1. # Aesthetic - 06/11/2019
The horse leave command was removed, in the horse command tier boost's are displayed as items. Removed the second page of shop, added life boost c and b was buffed, and shops was made to look more aesthetic.

1. # Minor update - 06/10/2019
Captcha's event rewards reduced by 75%, fixed bugs in the jail command and jails rewards were reduced.

1. # Update - 06/10/2019
This update included a almost complete rework of the captcha system making it cooler and more interactive with choices like being able to try to kill the guard after being jailed. The bot command was removed and 2 new events were added, megalodon(similar to epic tree) and epic coin. There were also some other minor changes.

1. # Minor update - 06/09/2019
Added a 30 minute cooldown to the items you can buy in the epic shop. Reduced daily and weekly cooldowns by 10 minutes.

1. # Epic shop - 06/09/2019
Added the epic shop where you can buy items with epic coins. Coin rain event was buffed and the amount of coins you get was changed to depend on the starters level.

1. # Minor update - 06/08/2019
Added the tractor and spelunk commands, the 3rd tier pickup and mine working commands. Epic quest rewards were buffed, balanced edgy armor and ultra log recipes, and other minor changes and fixes. 

1. # Area #12 - 06/08/2019
This update added area 12 and with the addition of area 12 came dungeon 11 with total different battle mechanics. The forge command was added and new equipment, along with other bug fixes and nerf of time travel xp boost.

1. # Random events - 06/06/2019
This update added random events you can find with a small chance through hunt, adventure and working commands and other minor changes.

1. # Minor update - 06/06/2019
Fixed bugs in the top command, new training minigame, new alias for training, and training xp nerf and adventure xp buff.

1. # Minor update - 06/04/2019
Blackjack and cups rewards were increased. 

1. # Minor balance - 06/04/2019
Arena balanced.

1. # Minor update - 06/04/2019
Now hunt and adventure says the name of your horse when it saves you from dying, arena balanced.

1. # Minor changes - 06/03/2019
Dungeon #8 dragon's life got reduced, trades for rubies reduced, jackpot balanced and sell prices were increased depending on the item.

1. # F - 05/31/2019
F command removed.

1. # Minor update - 05/31/2019
Jackpot only has one winner now and the inventory/inv command now works with i.

1. # Minor update - 05/31/2019
Arena now only depended on the owners cooldown. Miniboss, arena, and jackpot now automatically end when 10 players join, patreon buff applied to arena cooldown.

1. # Aesthetic time - 05/31/2019
The top command was completely remade including 5 pages to top coins. Added a leaderboard for time travels and another miniboss name and 2 new enchantments(omega and Ultra-Omega).

1. # Arena - 05/31/2019
The arena update added the arena command which had a cooldown of 8hrs, removed the pvp command, and pvp quests were replaced with arena quests. Also fixed many typos and bugs.

1. # Trade system - 05/29/2019
The trade command was remade and you no longer could trade with players, now you trade with an EPIC NPC. Trade rates were balanced and depended on your area and you couldn't trade items you didn't have access to.

1. # Jackpot is back - 05/29/2019
welcome back Jackpot. Added the jackpot command fixing all the bugs in the command, aesthetic improvements in jackpot. f is now a hidden command.

1. # Minor update - 05/28/2019
This update added the second mine command(Drill), buffed time travel bonus and reduced epic and edgy lootbox prices. Many other bug fixes as well.

1. # Minor update - 05/26/2019
Fixed bugs in horse and quest commands.

1. # Minor update - 05/25/2019
Added 3 new miniboss names and bug fixes in multiple commands.

1. # Minor update - 05/24/2019
Added a cool message when you die, chop commands gave more logs and other minor changes.

1. #  Horse update - 05/24/2019 
The horse update added tier 5 and 6 horses, levels also now average out in breed. capture command removed, horse cooldown reduced to 24hrs, and improved horse commands. top coins and top gc now have commas, equipment nerfs, baked fish buff and time travel bonus buff.

1. # Weekly - 05/23/2019
Added the weekly command, same as daily but with more rewards and weekly. Fixed bugs in sell, buy and overfish.

1. # Dismantle - 05/22/2019
Added the dismantle command and made craft, dismantle, buy, and sell case sensitive. Nerfed wooden sword, fish armor, and electronical armor recipes. Buffed wolf armor and epic armor recipes.

1. # Minor update - 05/22/2019
Changes with the cooldown, recipes and craft commands to make them easier to understand. Working command names were replaced with cooler names and some Minor bug fixes.

1. # Minor update - 05/20/2019
Miniboss rewards were increased by 15% and the chance to level up in a miniboss was increased from a 2% chance to 2.5%. Edgy sword recipe was nerfed and the f command was nerfed to only give 2-3 coins from 5-10 coins.

1. # Leaderboard update - 05/18/2019
Changed the top command to display only players from your server, you can still see top players of the bot with top gl or top gc. Top is now sorted by level and xp instead of only level, and you can see your rank from your profile.

1. # BIG UPDATE - 05/16/2019
This update was the biggest update since release coming with the ability to check other players cooldowns, new players now started with 500 coins, title improvements and time travel bonus buffs. You can now store lootboxes! You can find them in hunt or adventure with a low chance and can carry only 1 at a time. Other small changes like the guide command was replaced with the tutorial command and was rewritten, training xp was reduced by -5%, and mermaid armor was nerfed, and many other buffed fixes and Minor changes.

1. # Minor fix - 05/10/2019
Fixed a bug in 'balloons'.

1. # Minor changes - 05/07/2019
Fixed a bug in coinflip, removed unnecessary trades, now you can craft them! New recipes were buffed (50% -> 80%), and also fixed bugs in help, recipes and craft.

1. # Minor update - 05/05/2019
Added the customprofile command for ultra donators, and new tiers on patreon. Also Minor changes in profile, inventory, help and coinflip.

1. # Coinflip - 05/05/2019
Added the coinflip command, also trainings cooldown and rewards increased by 5x, and some other minor fixes.

1. # Minor update - 05/04/2019
Lootbox rewards were buffed along with a new command, training which gave you xp depending on your area but you had to complete a small mini game first.

1. # Refine - 04/30/2019
Added Refine, unlocked in area 7, same as enchant but will give better enchants and the cost is 10x enchant, descriptions in the help command were improved.

1. # Dungeon #10 and area 11 - 04/29/2019
Added Dungeon 10 and area 11, dungeon 10 was completely different from previous dungeons, also added the time travel command unlocked in area 11.

1. # Minor update - 04/26/2019
Added more trades, titles and some other Minor changes.

1. # Trade update - 04/23/2019
Trade commands added with a few trades, area tells you what you are doing wrong when the command isn't used correctly. Added an epic fish recipe.

1. # Minor update - 04/22/2019
Gambling commands were imported aswell as made dice look better, also cups reward was increased to 1.25x and bug fixes.

1. # More aesthetic - 04/21/2019
Made rpg profile look more appealing, and *EPIC player* was replaced with *normie player.* Stats and skills commands were replaced with, progress, stats, and equipment, and fixed a bug with marriage.

1. # Easter event - 04/20/2019
seasonal easter event (Ver: Easter). Added Easter eggs, you could bet them with egg slots, eat them with egg eat(healed you with a life boost) or use them in the egg shop. Titles were also added, EPIC player(unlocked when you breathe), and eggspert(bought with 1000 eggs in the egg shop).

1. # Minor update - 04/19/2019
Added lootboxes and removed chest, buying a lootbox gives a 3 hour cooldown, only 2 pages for swords/armor and bug fixes.

1. # Aesthetic time - 04/17/2019
Stats command is now called 'rpg profile' or 'p' aswell as made profile, heal, inv, and help look more appealing. This update also added coins, life, stats, and skills commands for epic/edgy donors aswell as marriage, divorce and take, for edgy donors.

1. # Minor update - 04/14/2019
added hunt and adventure events, and epic quest rewards were increased and damaged was reduced. People who helped with a miniboss was increased to 2x, f command now gave 5-10 coins instead of 2-5.

1. # Minor update - 04/13/2019
Help command was updated, now you can see how many coins you need to level up your horse with 'rpg horse' and the amount of coins needed was minorly decreased.

1. # Gambling time - 04/12/2019
Added the slots command, increased the amount of coins you get from blackjack from 50% of your bet to 75%. This also came with some bug fixes.

1. # Horse update - 04/11/2019
The horse command along was added I this update with 4 tiers of horse, horses gave attack, defense, hp or extra coins. Also added a capture command and epic quest, if you had a special horse.

1. # Quest update - 04/04/2019
The quest command was added in this update as well as making the stats look better and increased the amount of coins you obtain from mine.

1. # Jackpot update - 03/31/2019
The Jackpot command was added in this update as well as the give command.
