# Horse Festival

1. ## **Horse Festival 2024**
This event started on August 5, 2024 around 8:05 UTC and ends on August 20, 2024 at 23:55 UTC. After it ends, there is an extra week to use remaining event items in inventory.

This event introduces a lot of items and many benefits for your horse. It has a rare mob, megarace, and an event quest which can be completed to obtain an exclusive Pony Pet (Ver: Pets#Pony%20Pet)! During the whole event, <code>horse breeding</code> cooldown is reduced by **35%**.

1. #  Commands 

      - /hf info** - Gives information on various topics about this event.
      - /hf minirace** - Displays info about minirace. It's a small tournament.
      - /hf megarace** - Displays info about megarace and possible rewards.
      - /hf megarace start** - Joins a megarace.
      - /hf quest** - Shows your current progress with Horse Festival quest.
      - /hf tasks** - Shows daily and weekly tasks to complete for rewards.
      - /hf lightspeed** - Makes your horse ride at the speed of light, reducing all cooldowns (except <code>vote</code>) by half. This command uses your horse breeding/race cooldown.
      - /hf recipes** - Shows a list of recipes of horse festival items.
      - /hf craft [item] [amount]** - Craft horse festival items.
      - /hf melt** - Makes ingots from melted horseshoes and coins.
      - /hf shop** - Displays the horse festival shop.
      - /hf buy [item] [amount]** - Purchases an item from the horse festival shop.
      - /hf use [item]** - Uses a horse festival item.
      - /hf title [shop/megarace/quest]** - Equips a title.

1. #  Rare hunt mob
 A **horslime** is a rare mob found in <code>hunt</code>. You can increase your chance of encountering it by crafting a  cowboy sword and a  cowboy armor. Once killed, it drops  1 OMEGA horse token. If a player hunts together with their partner, both will get the reward.

1. # Megarace
You can join a megarace every week to get lots of rewards.

1. ## **Instructions** 
- Use <code>hf megarace start</code> to start a stage of this week's megarace.
- In each stage there will be a minigame with different paths that will lead you to the end of the stage.
- The amount of time you will have to wait until the next stage will depend on your choices.
- A  **horse armor** will increase your luck in your choices.
- The reward depends on the maximum stage achieved before the end of the week.
- The reward must be claimed during the next week after the megarace ended with <code>hf megarace</code>.
- Megaraces start at **00:05 UTC on Mondays** and ends at **23:55 UTC on Sundays**.

1. #  Minirace


A minirace is a horse tournament that was added in 2024.

    - OVERVIEW**
- Miniraces are 16 player knockout tournaments.
- There are 4 rounds in every minirace.
- Every round lasts up to one day.
- You can do one round a day.

    - HOW TO PLAY**
- Use <code>/hf minirace</code> to check your minirace or join one.
- The game will notify you When your round is ready.
- Use <code>/hf minirace</code> again to choose an action.
- Use <code>/hf minirace</code> after the round ended to see the players.

    - POSSIBLE ACTIONS**
- <code>RIDE</code> vs <code>SUS</code>: <code>RIDE</code> has higher chance to win.
- <code>SUS</code> vs <code>SPEEDRUN</code>: <code>SUS</code> has higher chance to win.
- <code>SPEEDRUN</code> vs <code>RIDE</code>: <code>SPEEDRUN</code> has higher chance to win.
- <code>RIDE</code> vs <code>RIDE</code>: Winner is RNG.
- <code>SUS</code> vs <code>SUS</code>: Both players lose.
- <code>SPEEDRUN</code> vs <code>SPEEDRUN</code>: Player with lower horse fatigue wins.
- If both players have the same value, winner is RNG.
- If your opponent doesn't answer, you win.

    - DEBUFFS**
- Suspiciousness lowers the chance to win using <code>SUS</code>.
- <code>SPEEDRUN</code> lowers your suspiciousness greatly.
- <code>RIDE</code> lowers your suspiciousness slightly.
- Horse fatigue lowers the chance to win using <code>SPEEDRUN</code>.
- <code>SUS</code> lowers your horse fatigue greatly.
- <code>RIDE</code> lowers your horse fatigue slightly.

    - WINNING A ROUND**
- If you win a round, you will stay in the minirace.
- You can fight the next round next day.
- If you lose a round, you will drop out of the minirace.
- You can join a new minirace next day.

    - WINNING THE TOURNAMENT**

- If you win a tournament, you advance a tier and get 1  sleepy potion.
- Higher tiered tournaments play exactly the same.
- If you manage to win a tier I tournament, you get the champion background (Ver: Background#Champion)
    - This is contrary to what the minirace text mentions

- Source: Epic RPG Guide*
