# Epicness

Epicness and Epic berry are horse related items that were added on 19/09/2022, they help buff and advance your horse even further!

1. ##  Epic Berries 
    - Epic Berries** are an item that helps your horse advance even further! They can be obtained through <code>**horse breeding**</code>, **pickup** and its advanced commands, and as a rare drop in **hunt** and **adventure** (0.01% base chance, affected by **time travels** and **other mob drop boosts**). They boost your chance of getting **Epicness** from **horse breeding.**

1. ##  Epicness 
Epicness system for Horses!

Each epicness you earn (/horse status) gives your horse a 0.5% (NOT flat, multiplier) bonus to its main stat! Every 5 epicness you have, your horse tier's bonuses are increased by 4% (mutliplier)

There is a "softcap" at 50 epicness, after the softcap it's really hard to get more epicness! But possible. (50 Epicness = 1.25x Main stat and 1.40x Tier Bonuses) 

You get a horse with more epicness through horse breeding: the chance depends on how many EPIC Berries you have in your inventory. Failing gives 2 ~ 3 berries, and succeeding at getting epicness halves the epic berries in your inventory.

Using a **GODLY Horse Token**  while having a Tier X Horse gives 50 EPIC Berries.

1. ###  Epicness stat boost examples 

15 Epicness with Tier X Horse gives you a x5.75 multiplier for getting pets in training rather than x5.

50 Epicness a Tier IX L92 Magic Horse gives you a 59.8% boost instead of 47.84%.
