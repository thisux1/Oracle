# Background

- /bg (alias: @EPIC RPG#4117 bg - background)** is a command to change the profile background.

1. # Profile Backgrounds
Profile backgrounds will replace the profile's embed with an image displaying the same stats.

It is possible to obtain profile backgrounds during Special Events (Ver: Special Events), by purchasing them in the Epic Shop (Ver: Shop#Epic Shop) using  Epic Coins, or reaching a certain number of  Time Travels (Ver: Time Travel). All backgrounds are purely cosmetic and grant no additional boosts / buffs in-game.

1. # Epic Shop Backgrounds
These are the backgrounds purchasable in the Epic Shop (Ver: Shop#Epic Shop), all of them costing the same  120 Epic Coins price.

1. ##  Blue 
Purchasable from the Epic Shop (Ver: Shop#Epic Shop) for 120  Epic Coins.


1. ##  Black 
Purchasable from the Epic Shop (Ver: Shop#Epic Shop) for 120  Epic Coins.


1. ##  Scroll 
Purchasable from the Epic Shop (Ver: Shop#Epic Shop) for 120  Epic Coins.


1. ##  Dungeon 
Purchasable from the Epic Shop (Ver: Shop#Epic Shop) for 120  Epic Coins.


1. ##  Space 
Purchasable from the Epic Shop (Ver: Shop#Epic Shop) for 120  Epic Coins.


1. ##  Ivy 
Now unobtainable, originally purchasable in the Epic Shop (Ver: Shop#Epic Shop) for 120  Epic Coins. Users who purchased this background and never sold it for 120  Epic Coins are still able to use it to this day.


1. # Time Travel Milestone Backgrounds
These are the backgrounds rewarded to the player after reaching a certain number of  Time Travels (Ver: Time Travel). At the moment, there are a total of 3 milestones.

1. ##  White 
Obtained automatically after reaching 50  Time Travels (Ver: Time Travel).


1. ##  Whitest 
Obtained automatically after reaching 100  Time Travels (Ver: Time Travel).


1. ##  Blackest 
Obtained automatically after reaching 150  Time Travels (Ver: Time Travel).


1. # Event Backgrounds
These are the backgrounds purchasable in the event's respective shops. The currency used to purchase them will also vary depending on the event. More information can be found on Special Events.

1. ##  Halloween 
Purchasable in the Halloween shop during the Halloween event using  Spooky Orbs.


1. ##  Christmas 
Purchasable in the Christmas shop during the Christmas event using  Gingerbread.

 

1. ##  Easter 
Purchasable in the Easter shop during the Easter event using  Golden Eggs.


1. ##  Horse 
Purchasable in the Horse Festival shop during the Horse Festival event using  Golden Horseshoes.


1. ##  Champion 
Obtained by winning a minirace during the Horse Festival event.


1. ##  Summer 
Purchasable in the Summer shop during the Summer event using  MEGA palm logs.
