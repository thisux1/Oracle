# Give

Give is a command used to give coins to another player.

1. #  Restrictions 

- After time travel #2, it is not possible to give coins to players that have not done the time travel #2
- After time travel #20, it is not possible to give coins to players that have not done the time travel #20  
    - There is also a maximum amount of coins that you can be given or multidiced depending on your area and Time Travel,** called coin cap (Ver: Coins Cap). 


1. #  Trivia 
During the april fools update you could steal coins by using this command, but the chance was low.
