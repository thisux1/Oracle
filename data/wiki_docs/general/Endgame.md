# Endgame

Hello once again, dear adventurer! So you've reached TT25, what we call the endgame. Now there will be new features wandering around waiting to be investigated by you, from weird blue cookies that make you travel in time to fighting wormholes or supernovas!?

1. #  Dungeon 15 and SUPER time travel 

1. ##  Dungeon 15 
First of all, we probably have to introduce you to the part 1 of Dungeon 15. Just so you start getting ready for it as soon as possible, you will need to have 3 T5+ pets of each type... Dungeon 15 also requires you to have the ULTRA-OMEGA equipment, which you will find out how much to farm for it soon enough. Also, you might want to have a T6+ horse, because the dungeon key is worth 500,000,000 coins! Beating this dungeon provides a TIME key, which you will need in order to super time travel. You can see a guide on how Dungeon 15 works here (Ver: Dungeon 15.1).

1. ##  SUPER time travel 
Now, the very much mentioned SUPER time travel (or how many people call it, STT). Can't do normal time travels anymore, nope. And you won't be selling your equipment or monster drops anymore either, as you will need them to get STT score. Everything that used to be lost in a time travel, now should be kept to increase this score and be able to afford a reward from the STT shop. The reward you choose, even if it is a simple pet or a GODLY lootbox, you will start with it in A1 in your next time travel. A fairly important recommendation is that you trade your materials like wooden logs or normie fish into rubies, since they give by far the most STT score.

1. ###  SUPER time travel guide 
For your first few STTs, you will be farming at least around 800,000 normie fish before leaving A3 (or its proportional in rubies, each ruby is worth 225 A3 fish), since you'll need it to afford the ULTRA-OMEGA equipment plus the ULTRA logs reward from the STT shop. Might sound like a lot, but your time travel boosts and your pets, the cute little companions that often provide materials, will surely help a bunch with that!

It might get monotonous since you'll be following this for a few runs, but worry not, as something even more boring is yet to come... the popular ultraining run! What is your main goal? To farm at least 3,000 coolness. Will it make you want to quit? Possibly. But don't worry, it will be worth it. 

1. #  Ultraining run 
We recommend to be at least TT35 to start your ultraining run and I'll explain it really quickly on what you'll be doing. A lot of stats to push a lot of stages in ultraining. A lot of coins come as a secondary reward. Access to the TOP, a closer relationship with the Epic NPC and VOID (many, many, many time travels!!). If that's not enough to interest you then I don't know what will.

Now comes the long and tedious explanation to have a smoother ultraining run. First of all, comes a choice. You will want to either choose the GODLY lootbox or the ULTRA logs from the STT shop. The latter will help you get the Edgy sword sooner but the former will allow you to make the GODLY sword and enter D15.2 in that same time travel, but be aware that you will also need to have 10 dragon essence (obtainable item from dungeons 1-9 once you have a Fighter pet). Once you get your Edgy sword in A1, either because you had the ULTRA log from the STT reward or because you got it from one of your amazing pets, you will want to enchant your sword to VOID. It will be cheaper than in higher areas. Then, the armor you'll probably aim for, will be the Lootbox armor (wow, finally a use for tryhard recipes), and you will be enchanting it to VOID as well. Don't worry about coins, as they will all be coming back from duels, votes and possibly even boosted minibosses to waste your dungeon cooldown once you start getting high levelled. You can start spamming the ultraining command in lower areas if you're able to, but the real deal comes once you reach A10 and cook apple juices, since that's when you will have a HUGE amount of stats. This time travel will be highly time consuming not because of the amount of fish you will need to farm in A3 to afford the apple juices, but rather because of the amount of time it takes to spam the ultraining (Ver: Training#Ultraining) command. TIME cookies can help speed up the process.

1. #  Ascending pets 
Pets... they're great, right? So obedient too, they bring you materials and, sometimes, even a lootbox or a monster drop! Well, let me tell you something that can be very fun. You will be getting a LOT more pets than what you're used to. After managing to get the 7 normal skills in a single pet, and SUPER time travelling for the first time, you unlock the command 'rpg pets ascend [pet ID]'. Ascending a pet gives it a chance (11.11% per rank, 100% at rank SS+) to bring back another pet from its adventure. But careful! Be sure to have a few skills ready to fuse into the pet after ascending it, especially Fast and Epic, you don't want the pet to be a burden for your other pets.

1. #  TOP and Dungeon 15 part 2 
Oh no! Did I mention the TOP? Well, now comes the time to explain it then. Firstly and most importantly... this is not an AREA! This is what you could call the Epic NPC's personal space, and you'll have the privilege to access it after defeating the second part of Dungeon 15 (Ver: Dungeon 15.2) (yes, there's a second part!). Once you got to A15, and before entering D15.1, you will want to forge the GODLY sword (you will have a GODLY lootbox from the STT shop, the ULTRA logs reward won't be as necessary anymore). After beating the first part of D15, it will take you to the second part, where the coolness you've worked so hard for now comes in clutch. If you manage to beat the dungeon, you will access the TOP and get TIME dragon essence... and a badge. Make sure to claim the badge. There are also a few new commands you can use, like 'rpg epic npc' or 'rpg void', check them out!

1. #  VOID 
Next step on your splendid adventure is the VOID (Ver: Void). It sounds kinda scary, I know right? Let's go straight to the point. 

1. ##  Introduction 
VOID is what we call areas 16-20. You can access them by moving with 'rpg area [area number]', but you must have an EPIC jump in your inventory (we'll shortly tell you how to get them) and the areas must be unsealed. There's a mysterious aura going around these areas, and each on its own has specific debuffs, you may check them at anytime with 'rpg void debuffs'. How to unseal an area? You may ask. Well, just go ahead to 'rpg void areas' and take a look at that. 

1. ##  How to get EPIC jumps? 
There are 2 ways. First and most important one is after fighting and defeating the Epic NPC, fight that you can enter after eating a GODLY cookie. Keep in mind that there are very important requirements to fighting the Epic NPC, like not having much materials in your inventory, selling a certain amount of wooden logs and having specific pets. After defeating the Epic NPC once, you will unlock the ability to buy EPIC jumps from the shop, its price is 2 TIME dragon essences.

1. ##  Benefits of VOID 
Well, as we mentioned before: many, many, many time travels. There is what we call TT bonuses, which you can obtain from time travelling in the VOID and from defeating D16-20. Time travelling in VOID areas gives an extra TT, for example time travelling in A16 would give +2 time travels instead of 1, time travelling in A17 would give +3 time travels instead of 1, and so on. Defeating D16-20 gives a permanent bonus of "getting an extra TT every certain amount of time travels". For D16 you will receive this extra TT every 5 TTs, for D17 it's 4 TTs, and so on.
