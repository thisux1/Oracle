# Dungeon 15.1

1. # **Introduction**
This is it. The final barrier between you and greatness, the TIME dragon is ready to make you suffer. So you gather a team.. *Our battle will be legendary!*

Dungeon 15 is unlocked once the player has traveled 25 times, and it will be required to defeat it in order to keep time travelling.. and unlocking the super time travel command.

The requirements to enter this dungeon are:

- ULTRA-OMEGA sword - Omega Sword + 50 ultra logs + 80 dragon scales
- ULTRA-OMEGA armor - Omega Armor + 1 Life potion + 300 dragon scales
- 25 time travels - You can see how many time travels you have done with **rpg time travel**
- A pet (Ver: Pets) of each standard type (Dog, Cat, Dragon) all at least Tier 5 and above
- Tier 6 or higher horse


- Both of these recips can be found in the command **rpg forge**

1. # The Board

The board has 8 columns/files and 8 rows/ranks, of which all except the square of the boss are playable. 

5 entities will be placed randomly on the board when starting:

- Player (Sword/Armor symbol)
- The TIME DRAGON
- Each of your 3 pets

You will randomly be placed on a square in the third row from the bottom in Sword mode. Your pets are also randomly placed, but with a few limitations. The dragon pet will spawn on a random square in the bottom two rows, the cat in the two rows beneath the dragon, and the dog in the top two rows. The TIME Dragon is even more restricted; it can only spawn on the third, fourth, fifth, or sixth tile of the third row from the top (and will stay there through out the entire dungeon).

1. #  How This Dungeon Works

To win, you need to attack the dragon once. To be able to attack it, it needs to be surrounded at all 4 sides by your "team" of pets. You need to reach this position with the moves available to you.

Every turn, the dragon hits you for 2 or 4 HP damage depending on what equipment you have selected.

You can switch between using the "Sword" or "Armor":

- Sword: Moves 2 fields with UP/RIGHT/LEFT/DOWN. You take 4 damage each turn while in this form.
- Armor: Moves only a single field with UP/RIGHT/LEFT/DOWN. You take 2 damage each turn while in this form.

1. # The Pets

You are only able to control yourself. The pets will follow their own logic which depends on the amount of HP you currently have. There are four different pet phases, each with different lengths:

- 200 - 131:
Cat: moves towards the dragon, 1 block at a time, though when diagonal with the dragon it will move 1 block up or down.

Dog: it has a complicated pattern, which is explained below the other phases.

Dragon: mirrors you using you as the reference point, doing the exact move on the opposite side.
- 130 - 101:
Cat: moves down 3 blocks. 

Dog: switches positions with the cat. 

Dragon: will not move at all.
- 100 - 61:
Cat: will move in the opposite direction 1 block away at a time. If confused by direction or trapped, it will not move at all. 

Dog: moves up 3 blocks. 

Dragon: will move between the opposite block between the one it's sitting on currently.
- 60 - 0:
Cat: moves 3 blocks to the right. 

Dog: switches position with the dragon. 

Dragon: moves 3 spaces left.

This is the pattern for the Dog's first phase:

- 1,1 tile is the one in the top left corner.

- dog tile is the tile the dog is currently on.

- it will first try the priority movement, if the tile it tried to move on its blocked, it tries the secondary option, if even that is blocked, it won't do anything.
