# Dungeon 13

1. #  **Introduction** 
You've come so far and now you've reached dungeon 13, hats off to you! You finally get to experience another fun and relatively easy dungeon, so long as you get the hang of it~

Dungeon 13 is unlocked after your fifth time travel, and you will need to beat it in order to keep advancing time travels and unlocking the next area. 

The requirements to enter this dungeon are:

- **D13 key** - Purchasable in the shop for 100,000,000 coins. You can avoid buying the key with a Tier 6+ horse.
- At least **5 time travels** - You can check how many time travels you have with the command **rpg time travel**
- **Omega sword** - 50 mega logs + Ultra-Edgy sword + 30 dragon scales + At least 1001+ health. You can see this recipe with **rpg forge**

Note: Your max health won't decrease upon forging the Omega sword, you will just receive the damage. So, it is possible to have a 951 HP + Life boost C to save a few materials.

1. # How it works
The dungeon is trivia themed and has a series of questions which will be asked randomly. The player needs to answer these questions with correct, not so correct, and wrong answers. The player also needs to move either left, right, or center. 

Only a few aspects of the dungeon are random; these aspects being the question in each room  (the order of the questions asked and their answers) and the initial room number (which will be either 1, 2, or 3 and always 5 rooms away from the dragon).

Room numbers are not random and are dictated by how many rooms you move closer to or further from the dragon depending on your choice of correct vs. not so wrong or wrong answers. Here are the equations where X = current room number, Y = new room number and Z = rooms away after answer:
| Answer | Result | Initial Room Number (X) | New | Rooms Away (Z) | Additional |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Correct |  |  | X + 2 |  |  |
| Correct |  |  | X / 2 |  |  |
| Not so wrong |  |  | X + 7 |  |  |
| Not so wrong |  |  | X - 1 |  |  |
| Wrong |  |  | X - 1 |  | If Z > 7 |
| Wrong |  |  | X + 4 |  | If Z < 4 |
| Wrong |  |  | X |  | If 3 < Z < 8 |

Damage is calculated as Z*2 (e.g. room #5 and 9 rooms away → 18 damage)

Distance from the dragon is affected by normal movement values.

1. #  Questions and Answers 
Above are the 15 questions and the correct, not so wrong, and wrong answers that correlate with them.

With the Super-Time-Travel Update, the question "What is the cap in time-travels?" was changed:

- None: Wrong

- 10: Not so wrong

- Very high: Correct

With the 2021 Valentine Update, the question "How many vehicles are there?" was changed:

- 4: Not so wrong

- 7: Correct
1. #  Tips & Tricks 
There are several winning paths (room # , distance) that the players can approach from.

This dungeon is not based on whether or not you can get every question correct; players should instead strategically alternate between choosing a correct, not so wrong, or wrong answer.

Raise your room number before actually attempting to get closer to the dragon by answering Not so wrong and Wrong. To start approaching the dragon, continue with Wrong answers until you are less than 8 rooms from the dragon and your room number is 12 or more. Then answer Not so wrong until your room number is exactly 8. You should now be less than 4 rooms away, so by answering Correct a few times you will reach the dragon room and can Attack. 

    - Correct**-Even room numbers move 1 room closer to the dragon and odd numbers move 2 rooms away. **Not so wrong**-Room numbers less than 9 move 4 rooms away from the dragon and numbers greater than 8 move 1 room closer. **Wrong**-Move 2 rooms closer when the dragon is within 8 or more rooms, 5 rooms away when the dragon is within 3 or less rooms, and no movement for 7 through 4.

1000HP is more than 6 times as much as you need to win; you do not need to waste resources on increasing this.
