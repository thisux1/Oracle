# Dungeon 12

1. #  **Introduction** 
You have mastered the art of the first solo dungeon and now the EPIC God comes to you with a new challenge. The Omega Dragon is raised inside this dungeon trying to stop you, but will it be able to do it?

Dungeon 12 is only attemptable after time traveling three times and you will need to beat it in order to keep travelling through time. 

If you think that forging the Ultra-Edgy Sword was not that bad, from now on you will have to forge Ultra-Edgy Armor too, so sharpen your Sword and go hunt some dragons!


The requirements to enter this Dungeon are:
      - D12 Key** - Purchasable in the shop (50,000,000 Coins). You can avoid having to buy it by having a T6+ Horse.
- At least **3 time travels** - You can check how many time travels you have done using the command "**rpg time travel**".
      - Ultra-EDGY Armor** - Level 70 + 1 Ultra Log + 400 Ruby + 20 Dragon Scale

    - Note:** You do not need the Ultra-EDGY Sword as well to enter, which is useful to know in case the player has either accidentally sold their sword or in the case of seasonal events, used a limited-time only event item to skip areas.

1. #  **How does it work** 
This dungeon is based around movement, HP, defense and collecting 10 orbs. You will find yourself in the center of a 3x3 board represented by the Ultra-Edgy Armor.

This time, tiles of the board will be different between 4 options:
- Little white square: Stepping on it will heal you for 5 HP but you will lose 5 Energy Orbs.
- Black square with white borders: Stepping on it will hit you for 500 HP.
- Black square: Stepping on it will give you 1 Energy Orb and will hit you for 25 HP.
- Big White square: Stepping on it won´t make anything but you need to stand on it to attack once you have 10 orbs.

Every turn you will have to choose between going up, down, left or right, or attack if you fit all the requirements.

In addition, every single turn you will be hit for 30 to 60 HP depending on your defense stat value.

Tiles will change every single turn following the pattern: Little White > Black with white borders > Black > Big White > Little white > ...

However, the tile you moved to does not change until another movement.

To defeat the Omega Dragon you will need to collect 10 Energy Orbs, step on a white tile and attack.


1. #  **Tips & Tricks** 
-Being in the center will let you move in all 4 directions, being on a border will let you move in 3 different directions and being in a corner will let you move only in 2 different directions. It is recommended to not go to the corners unless if it is totally necessary. Try to foresee the next two options before you move to a corner and only proceed if there is at least one safe move.

-Little white squares are a good option at the beginning of the dungeon. Once you have collected a few Energy Orbs, it becomes one of the worst options since it makes you lose a lot of progress and it heals for almost nothing. Black squares with white borders should be avoided as much as possible.

-The dragon deals minimal damage (30 hp per turn) when you have 700 def or more. This does not include the damage from tiles. You can get recommended DEF by having the Ultra-EDGY Armor with a Perfect enchant or above. Also, it is recommended to have at least 851 Base HP + Life boost C = 901 HP in order to beat it.  

    - Note:** Having more HP increases the chances of success of clearing the Dungeon  

    - Other notes: -**D12 is RNG-based when you say "yeee" and start the dungeon   

-Don't attempt with less than 700 defense, you need to make sure you only take 30 damage per turn from the dragon, if you less than 700 defense, you may take 31 damage and it will make your dragon harder, since you have less hp left than normal
