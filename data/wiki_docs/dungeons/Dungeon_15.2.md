# Dungeon 15.2

After 25+ Time Travels (Ver: Time Travel), the first possibility to enter Part 2 of Dungeon 15 becomes available. You first need to "defeat" the TIME DRAGON in Dungeon 15.1 to advance; successful completion of Dungeon 15.1 is immediately followed by starting Dungeon 15.2 with healing the TIME DRAGON with no break.

The Time Dragon can only be killed by another Time Dragon. Succeeding at the 2nd part of the dungeon advances your area to the "TOP". You also receive the TIME KEY, which is required for Super Time Travel (Ver: Time Travel#Super%20Time%20Travel).

This is Part 2 of the Dungeon.

1. ##  **Requirements** 

- GODLY sword (Level 500+ Requirement)
- A pet (Ver: Pets) of each <ins>standard</ins> type (Dog, Cat, Dragon) <ins>all</ins> at least Tier 5 and above
- Tier 6+ horse
- 2,000+ Coolness*

    - Consider the requirements of the equipment in your run: You need 53 Ultra logs, 10 Dragon Essence, 13 (12(GODLY Sword)+1(UO Armor)) Omega Lootboxes, and 1 Godly Lootbox, so make sure to farm enough materials in A3/A5 and plan accordingly!**

    - Important Note: You must forge the GODLY sword BEFORE entering Dungeon 15.1, as you will not be able to forge it between Parts 1 and 2!**

Stats are not required for this Dungeon. You will start Part 2 with the HP you end Part 1 at. AT and DEF do not matter.

- * The amount of coolness varies, the more you have the more you will heal the dragon, this amount is only a recommendation. Having a lower amount may cause you to lose on time.

1. ##  **Mechanics:** 
The playable board is a 6 x 6 area, inside of an 8 x 8 purple border.

11 entities will be placed on the board when starting:

- You (GODLY sword icon)
- The TIME DRAGON (will start in the same position as it was in part 1, but will move)
- 9 red circles (used to heal yourself, and increase the TIME DRAGON's max HP)

To win, you need to over heal the TIME DRAGON. In order to heal the dragon, you must be standing directly next to it, and use the **Heal** command. The amount you heal him for is equal to 1% of your total coolness.

The TIME DRAGON will move away from you 1 tile at a time, prioritizing moving up/down rather than left/right. If the dragon reaches the end of the map, it will teleport itself onto a random tile on the board. If the dragon teleports 5 times, it will become dizzy and do nothing for 3 turns. There is also a chance for the TIME DRAGON to travel back in time 2-4 turns, undoing his moves from said turns, while also reverting any healing you may have previously done to him. Everything from said turns will be reset, except for the red circle pattern.

The red circles on the board will heal you for 8HP if you end your turn on one, but will increase the TIME DRAGON's max HP by +25 if he steps on one. The circles move in a set pattern. They will always stay in the same shape, but will shift towards different corners of the board. The pattern is as follows: TOP LEFT > TOP RIGHT > BOTTOM LEFT > BOTTOM RIGHT > TOP LEFT > TOP RIGHT, etc.

1. ##  **Strategy / Tips:** 

- Time is your #1 concern. Moving fast and staying aware are of upmost importance. Most failed runs end due to lack of time.
- Keep to the middle, while moving onto red circles. Try to make the dragon go towards the edge of the board while healing yourself, resulting in the dragon teleporting. This will eventually cause him to be dizzy after 5 teleports, giving yourself time to heal him. Only attempt to heal him if he is not too far away, don't go over for just a single heal.
- While healing the dragon is the ultimate goal, it is very important to focus on healing yourself as well. Stay above 100HP preferably, (150HP if you survived part 1 with 150+) and above 50HP at all costs. The TIME DRAGON tends to travel back in time more often when your HP is very low (20-50), making your recent healing towards him useless. Prioritize healing yourself over healing the dragon. 
- Use the dragons movement to your advantage, avoid letting him go into the red circles.

    - Note:** Don't forget to dismantle epic fish and below, bananas, and trade fish / apples / ruby's to logs due to TOP area having different trade rates. If you already have enough STT score, opening extra lootboxes is also recommended.
