# Dungeon 11

1. #  **Introduction** 
You did it once and now you have done it again, congrats! After doing your first time travel, things have become more interesting, letting you attempt the EPIC Solo Dungeons!

Dungeon 11 is unlocked after your first time travel, and you will need to beat it in order to do more time travels as well as unlock higher area dungeons. 

You have also unlocked a new command, **/forge**, the first time you reached area 11, so it is the perfect moment to make use of it.

The requirements to enter this dungeon are:
      - D11 Key** - Purchasable in the shop (25,000,000 coins). You can avoid having to buy it by owning a T6+  horse.
- At least **1 time travel** - You can check how many time travels you have done by using the command "**rpg time travel**".
- **Ultra-EDGY Sword** - Level 70 +  Edgy Sword +  Ultra Log + 10  Epic fish + 10  Dragon Scale.

1. #  **How does it work** 
This dungeon is based around movement, HP and attack. You will find yourself on an 8x8 board where you are represented by your most recent acquisition, the Ultra-Edgy Sword, located at the bottom left of the board while the ULTRA-EDGY Dragon will be located at the top right of the board.

Each turn, you will have to choose between moving up, down, right, left, pass turn or attack (you can only attack when you are standing next to the dragon). Also, each turn the dragon will shoot fireballs from the top of the board.

Being hit by a fireball will deal 100 HP flat damage.

Doing pass turn will hurt for 10 HP.

After each turn, every tile of the board will move down 1 line, and the lowest row line will disappear.

ULTRA-EDGY Dragon has 1000 HP, so attacking for less than that amount will hurt it and you will be sent back to a random tile in the bottom-left part of the board.

If you are at the side of the board and choose an option which goes to a direction with no space to land on, it will prevent the command from activating as it notices that there is no space to land on (for example, you cannot move left if you have already gone as far left as possible).

1. #  **Tips & Tricks** 
The movement being restricted to 4 ways or keeping still, it is nearly impossible to avoid every single fireball; thus, considering being hit a few times, the minimum HP recommended to face this dungeon is 681 HP base + Life Boost C = 731 HP. That makes you able to be hit 7 times and pass 3 turns before dying. To increase your HP base stat, you can cook baked fish (+5 HP) or fruit salad (+1 HP). [See cooking].

Achieving 1000 attack value to one shot the dragon is a nightmare. Having 499 attack or less means that you have to travel to the dragon 3 times, and having 500 - 999 attack means that you have to travel to the dragon 2 times. To reach 500 attack at level 70, it is necessary to enchant Ultra-EDGY Sword with at least the mega enchant.

Moving down will not help in any way, because the fire tiles will move down with you. The only way to avoid fireballs is moving left or right. It is recommended to prioritize moving up or moving to the right than moving left or choosing to pass a turn. Only move to the left or pass a turn if you can clearly see that it will be better in the next moves.

    - Moving right or left (without getting hit) while the fire moves down is effectively an up/diagonal move. You will move left/right on your screen, but up/diagonally on the board. So long as you do this from/to a safe space, you will not take damage, even if that safe space is entirely surrounded by fire.**

1. #  Trivia 

- ULTRA-EDGY Dragon is the only boss that doesn't attack the player itself, but only throws fire at them.
- It is possible to enter the dungeon with Omega Sword and Ultra-Omega Sword too.
- Armor and DEF stats are entirely ignored for this dungeon, meaning you could still enter this dungeon and fight it like usual with a low tier armor or without any armor at all.
- If you pass a turn while under a fireball, you will take damage from both and lose 110 HP in a single turn.
- This is the best dungeon to get the <code>WHY</code> title <code>(#77) (Achievements</code>) - get either 999 ATK, hit once, and intentionally lose
