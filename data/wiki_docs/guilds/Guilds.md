# Guilds

Sad that your party disbanded right after a dungeon? Wish there was a more lasting and social aspect to the game? Well look no further as the game has added a system just for you: Guilds!

Unlockable in area 4, the Guild system is a way for players to group up with their friends to go against other guilds in a weekly contest of raids for hopes of winning grand rewards! 

Guild names must be 2-12 letters and there is a maximum of 10 players per guild. There is also a max of 10 invites per week. 

You can check the top guilds with **/top guilds** (Level and Experience) or **/guild ranking** (Energy and Stealth)

Guild ranking results with weekly stats will be posted on the support server every Saturday at 22:00 UTC. Guild stats and rankings will reset as well.

The area requirement for guilds does not apply if the player has already time traveled.
1. # Dueling
Your guild will sometimes earn experience from each members' duels (Ver: Duel). Guild rewards from duels are not guaranteed; the closer the level of the two players dueling, the higher the chance of gaining rewards. The level difference to get 3 guild xp is x0.5-x2, meaning that if you duel someone that is half your level or two times your level, you will receive 3 guild xp. Level range for getting 1-2 guild xp is unknown. 

Keep in mind, dueling members of your guild will not yield any guild rewards.
1. # Guild Raid
Every 2 hours, Guilds are able to raid other guilds in order to obtain energy points that are used to determine weekly rankings on the ladder (**/guild ranking**). Any member can initiate a guild raid; however, the cooldown is shared amongst the whole guild as well as with the guild upgrade command so choose wisely.

Guild raid picks a random guild to raid depending on stealth and energy of other guilds, mostly it randomly finds a guild with lower stealth and higher energy. 

Initiating the guild raid will display a series of actions describing how the player is attacking the other guild as well as the guild's health. A max of three attack attempts will be listed, either ending in individual failure or success per attack; but so long as the last attack ends in 0/100, the player's guild has won.

If successful, the guild can earn anywhere from 30 - 65% (with a minimum of 1) of the enemy guild's energy. The guild that was raided will lose some energy, usually 1/4 of what the guild on the offense has earned. If a raid is unsuccessful, your guild will still loot some energy depending on the damage dealt and it can be as low as 0. Your guild will earn experience regardless.
1. # Guild Upgrade
Instead of raiding others, Guilds may choose to spend their cooldown on upgrading their stealth. Each upgrade boosts the Guild's stealth by 1 - 7, capping at 95 stealth. Guilds with higher stealth gain less stealth from upgrades and likewise, guilds with lower stealth have higher chance to gain much stealth from upgrades.

- Note: once you reach a high level, there's a chance that a guild upgrade will fail. Giving 0 stealth as it fails.*

Stealth determines how likely your guild is to be found when other guilds attempt a raid.

1. # **Guild Tasks**
Want to keep helping your guild aside from upgrading and raiding constantly? Bored from doing the same commands? Well fear not! 1. # Benefits of Guilds
Aside from enjoying an environment with friends and guildmates, there are some benefits a player gains from guilds. The major guild benefit is XP and coin rewards boost in duels. Each guild level adds 2% extra XP and coins to player's duel rewards when they win a duel. This bonus stacks with time travel XP boost and donor XP/coins boost and can grant massive amount of XP to players; sometimes enough to push them a few levels at once. Aside from the main guild benefits, there is a guild shop with useful items sold for guild rings.

1. # Guild Roles
There are two roles in a guild: member and owner. When a guild is created, the founder is the owner of the guild by default, they can invite other players into the guild. New invitees will enter the guild as members. **There is a 24 hour cooldown after leaving a guild** before you can join another guild (or the same guild). There is also a 24 hour cooldown between deleting a guild and creating a new one.

    - Owner:** Aside from upgrading, raiding and earning guild XP, guild owner can invite members with **/guild invite @mention**, kick members with **/guild kick @mention** (may use user id in this case), rename guild with **/buy guild name** (and write the new guild name when it asks for; costs 3 epic coins), transfer guild ownership with **/guild change owner @mention** (may be done to members of the guild only; new owner has to accept) and delete guild with **/guild delete** (can only be done when owner is alone in their guild). Guild owner can **not** leave their own guild. There can be only one owner in a guild.

    - Member**: Guild member can upgrade guild or initiate guild raids, freely leave and join/create other guilds. Guild members can win duels against non guildmates to earn EXP for the guild. Losers of a duel also have a chance to gain EXP for the guild. Guild members can purchase items from the guild shop using rings. There may be as many as 10 members in a guild (including the owner).
